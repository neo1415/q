# Privacy Policy

> **In short**
>
> - We collect what you give us (account, organisation, documents, voice, meetings) and how you use the service.
> - We use it to run the service for you, keep it secure, and meet the law. We do not sell it.
> - Q uses AI providers to process your data. They act on our instructions and may not use it to train their own models.
> - Your information goes to another user only when you choose to share it.
> - You can ask to see, download, correct or delete your data. Deleting your account removes your content. Records the law or other people need are kept, with your identity removed.
> - You can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or your local data protection authority.

## 4. How Q and AI providers use your data

Q is our AI assistant. To answer you, Q sends the relevant part of your data to AI providers that process it for us:

- AI model providers, for text, analysis and summaries;
- speech providers, to turn speech into text and text into speech;
- a video-avatar provider, for Q's face in rehearsals and meetings;
- a meeting-bot provider, which joins calls to transcribe them;
- search and research providers, for public web information.

They act as our processors under written terms. They may process data only to provide their service to us, and we choose providers whose terms do not allow them to train their models on our customers' data.

Before Q uses any data, we filter it by who is asking. Q only works with what the current user is allowed to see. A founder's private information is not used to shape what an investor sees unless the founder has shared it with them.

## 5. Who we share it with

- **Other users, at your choice.** For example: your profile with investors who fit, your data room with an investor you accept, a meeting record with the meeting's other side. Your visibility settings control this.
- **Your organisation.** Team members in your workspace see what your organisation's settings allow.
- **Service providers (processors)**: hosting and database, AI and speech, video, meeting bot, search, email delivery, malware scanning, payments, customer support. A current list is available on request at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.
- **Professional advisers**, such as lawyers, auditors and insurers, under confidentiality.
- **Authorities**, where the law requires it or to protect rights, safety and security.
- **A buyer or successor**, if our business is sold or merged, under the same protections.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

## 8. Security

We protect personal data with technical and organisational measures that include:

- encryption in transit and at rest;
- access rules enforced in the database, so each user and organisation can reach only what they are allowed;
- two-step verification for accounts;
- malware scanning of every uploaded file before it can be opened;
- logging and review of staff access to customer data, which is allowed only for support, security or legal reasons;
- limiting what each AI request can see to what that user may access.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the regulator within 72 hours of becoming aware of it where the law requires, and tell you without undue delay where the risk is high.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

- **access** your personal data and get a copy;
- **portability**: receive your data in a common, machine-readable format, or have it sent to another provider;
- **correction** of inaccurate or incomplete data;
- **deletion** of your data;
- **restriction** of how we use it, for example while a dispute is resolved;
- **objection** to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
- **withdraw consent** at any time, where we rely on consent. This does not affect earlier processing;
- not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that significantly affect you;
- **complain** to a supervisory authority. In Nigeria this is the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). In the EU it is your local data protection authority; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office.

To use these rights, use the options in your account settings where available, or write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We may need to confirm your identity. We answer within one month, or tell you if we need longer, as the law allows. Using your rights is free unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

## 10. How deleting your account works

When you delete your account:

- your profile, documents, conversations with Q, what Q remembers about you, and your other content are deleted;
- your access ends and any connected accounts are disconnected;
- if you are the only owner of an organisation's workspace, you are asked to transfer it or delete it too.

Some records cannot simply disappear, because the law requires them or because they also belong to other people. These are kept, with your identity removed or replaced by a neutral label:

- **audit and security records**, which show what happened without saying who you were;
- **other parties' relationship history**, for example that an investor met a company, so their own record stays accurate;
- **messages you sent to others**, which stay in the recipient's records, attributed to a former user;
- **invoices and tax records**, kept for the legal period.

We tell you what is kept and why when you delete your account. Data in backups is overwritten on the normal cycle.
