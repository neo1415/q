# Frequently asked questions

## For founders

### What do I need to get started?

Upload what you already have: a pitch deck, accounts, a data pack. There is no long questionnaire first. Q reads your material, shows you what it found and how well supported each fact is, and then asks only about the gaps. You can skip any question.

### Is the InvestIQ diagnosis really free?

Yes. The full diagnosis of your company, covering strengths, gaps, risks and readiness, is free on every plan and never goes behind a paywall.

### What happens if I don't know a figure, or don't want to share it?

Leave it. Unknown stays unknown. It is never turned into a zero or counted against you. Missing evidence lowers how certain the picture is; it does not mean your company is weak.

### Does Q change my figures without asking?

When Q reads a figure from a document, it asks you to confirm it before it becomes part of your profile. When you tell Q a figure about your own company directly, Q records it as your statement, tells you what it recorded, and lets you correct or undo it at once.

### Who can see my company's information?

You choose. Each piece of information has a visibility setting. Information you keep private never shapes what investors are shown or how they rank you. Your data room opens to an investor only after you accept their interest.

### How do I reach investors?

Investors who fit see your company in their feed, ordered by fit and evidence. You can also apply directly through an investor's GateQ gateway. When Q prepares an introduction, you see the exact message and who it goes to, and nothing is sent until you approve it.

### Can I practise my pitch?

Yes. You can pitch out loud to Q in a rehearsal. Q listens, asks the questions an investor would, and reviews you afterwards.

## For investors

### How long does set-up take?

A few minutes. Tell Q your stage, sectors, geography and cheque size, by typing or by voice. Skip anything you like. Your personalised feed is ready straight away.

### How is my feed ordered?

By your declared mandate, fit, evidence and freshness. Payment, views, watch time and who you know never count. The reasons sit on every pitch, so you can see why a company is there.

### Will what I browse change my mandate?

No. Viewing is not interest, and what you look at never silently rewrites the mandate you declared. Only you change your mandate.

### Do founders see when I pass?

A pass is recorded on the relationship, but your reason stays private to you.

### What is GateQ?

GateQ is your own intake gateway. Founders apply through a link you share. Q interviews them, collects their evidence, and checks each application against your published criteria. Your inbox sorts applications by fit, with every answer and document behind them. Q prepares a recommendation; you decide.

### Can my team work together?

Yes. Invite colleagues to your organisation's workspace. You share one record per company, and you can assign and add notes to GateQ applications.

## Privacy and security

### Do you use my documents to train AI?

No. We do not use your content to train general-purpose AI models, and we choose AI providers whose terms do not let them train on our customers' data.

### Who can see the documents I upload?

You, the people in your organisation your settings allow, and anyone you choose to share them with. Q works only with what the person asking is allowed to see. Every upload is scanned for malware before it can be opened.

### Where is my data stored?

Mainly in the European Union. Some providers we use, such as AI and speech services, may process data in other countries, under safeguards described in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

### Does Q record my meetings?

Only meetings it is asked to join. Q announces itself when it joins, appears in the call as a note-taker, and keeps the record for both sides. The organiser can decline recording or remove Q at any time. See our [Meeting Recording Notice](/legal/meeting-recording).

### Can I download or delete my data?

Yes. You can ask for a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account, from your settings where available or by writing to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Deleting your account removes your content. Records that the law or other people need, such as audit records or another party's relationship history, are kept with your identity removed, and we tell you what is kept and why.

### Can I control what Q remembers about me?

Yes. In Settings, under Privacy, you can see what Q remembers and remove anything you don't want it to keep.

## Plans and billing

### What does a plan include?

Every plan can ask Q, bring Q to meetings and get the full InvestIQ diagnosis. A plan sets how much use is included each month, such as questions, voice minutes and research jobs. See [Pricing](/pricing).

### What happens when I run out?

Q tells you when you reach 80% of an allowance, and again at 100%, when it offers a top-up or an upgrade. It never stops an answer halfway through.

### Do you take a percentage of money I raise or invest?

No. Plans pay for the software. Any fee on a deal would only ever apply under a separate written agreement.

### Does money for investments go through {{COMPANY_NAME}}?

Never. We do not hold, move or escrow investment money. Founders and investors move money directly between themselves. {{COMPANY_NAME}} only keeps a record of commitments and transfers that the parties enter.

### Can someone pay to be ranked higher?

No. There is no paid placement on any plan.

### How do I cancel?

In your plan settings, at any time. You keep your paid allowance until the end of the period, then move to the free plan.

## Q and AI

### What is Q?

Q is the AI analyst inside {{COMPANY_NAME}}. It reads the evidence, answers questions, prepares messages and next steps, and shows where every fact comes from. You talk to one Q, by typing or by voice.

### Can Q be wrong?

Yes. Q is an AI and can make mistakes. It shows the source and support behind each fact, marks its own inferences, and keeps conflicting figures side by side instead of picking one. Check anything important before you rely on it. Nothing Q says is investment, legal, tax or financial advice.

### Will Q send anything without asking me?

No. Q prepares the message and shows who it goes to. You approve that exact message, then it is sent. If it changes, it comes back to you for approval.

### Can Q read my email or calendar?

Only if you connect them. Connecting Google lets Q book calls on your calendar and, if you choose, read email you allow. You can disconnect at any time in Settings.

### Is Q's face a real person?

No. Q's voice and video face are generated by AI. The product says so wherever they appear.
