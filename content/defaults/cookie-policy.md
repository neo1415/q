# Cookie Policy

> **In short**
>
> - We use a small number of strictly necessary cookies to sign you in and keep your account secure.
> - We store a few preferences in your browser, such as light or dark mode, so the app remembers them.
> - Analytics tracking is off by default. It is used only if you turn it on.
> - We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not let third parties track you across other sites.

<!-- LAWYER: Confirm against the UK PECR, the EU ePrivacy Directive and the NDPA consent rules. If analytics is later added, consent must be collected before it runs. -->

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in the same way. In this policy "cookies" covers both.

## 2. The cookies we use

### Strictly necessary (always on)

These are needed for the Service to work. Without them you cannot sign in or use your account securely. The law does not require consent for them.

| Purpose               | What it does                                                            | How long                                   |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Sign-in session       | Keeps you signed in and protects your session                           | Until you sign out, or the session expires |
| Two-step verification | Remembers that you completed the second sign-in step on this device     | Short-lived                                |
| Workspace choice      | Remembers which workspace (founder or investor) you chose after sign-up | Up to one year                             |
| Security              | Helps prevent forged requests and abuse                                 | Session                                    |

### Preferences (always on, stored only in your browser)

These remember choices you make. They are not sent to us for tracking and are not shared with anyone.

| Purpose    | Examples                                             | How long                             |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Appearance | Light or dark mode, how much Q's light moves         | Until you clear your browser storage |
| Layout     | Pinned items, collapsed menus, workspace layout      | Until you clear your browser storage |
| Discover   | Your filters, captions setting and place in the feed | Until you clear your browser storage |
| Q          | Whether Q reads answers aloud                        | Until you clear your browser storage |

### Analytics (off unless you turn it on)

If you allow it, we measure how features are used so we can improve them. Analytics is first-party: the data is collected by us, not by an outside tracker, and it does not include the content of your documents or conversations. We keep it for up to 13 months.

### Advertising

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## 3. Third-party services

When you connect a third-party service, such as Google sign-in, or use a feature run by a provider, such as a video player or a payment page, that provider may set its own cookies under its own policy. Payment pages are hosted by our payment processors.

## 4. Your choices

- Change your analytics choice at any time in the cookie settings link in the footer.
- You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary cookies, you will not be able to sign in.
- Clearing your browser storage resets your preferences on that device.

## 5. Changes

We will update this policy if we change the cookies we use. The date at the top shows the latest version.
