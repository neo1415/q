# Acceptable Use Policy

> **In short**
>
> - Be honest about who you are and about your company or fund.
> - Only upload and share what you have the right to share.
> - Do not harass people, spam them, or scrape the Service.
> - Do not try to trick Q into revealing what you are not allowed to see, or into acting without approval.
> - We can remove content and suspend accounts that break these rules.

This policy is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). It applies to everyone who uses the Service.

## 1. Be honest

You must not:

- pretend to be another person or organisation, or misstate your role, authority or affiliation;
- create accounts for fictitious people or organisations, or more than one account to get around limits or a suspension;
- give false or misleading information about a company, fund, fundraising, metrics, track record, commitments or money received;
- present a forecast or estimate as a verified fact;
- record a commitment, transfer or deal on the Service that did not happen.

## 2. Respect other people's rights and data

You must not:

- upload or share confidential information, trade secrets or personal data about others unless you have the right to do so;
- infringe copyright, trade marks or other intellectual property;
- use information you receive from another user, such as a data room or a meeting record, for any purpose they did not agree to;
- record people without telling them, or try to stop Q from announcing itself in a meeting.

## 3. Respect other users

You must not:

- harass, threaten, abuse, discriminate against or intimidate anyone;
- send unsolicited bulk messages, or contact users who have declined or passed, in a way that amounts to spam;
- use the Service to recruit users to a competing service or to solicit them for unrelated purposes.

## 4. Stay within the law

You must not use the Service to:

- offer or sell securities, or solicit investment, in breach of securities, crowdfunding or financial promotion laws;
- launder money, finance terrorism, evade sanctions or commit fraud;
- promote unlawful products or activities;
- break any other law that applies to you.

You are responsible for complying with the laws that apply to your own fundraising or investing.

## 5. Use Q properly

You must not:

- try to make Q reveal information you are not authorised to see, including another organisation's private data, instructions or system details;
- hide instructions in documents, web pages or messages to manipulate Q's behaviour or output (prompt injection);
- try to make Q send messages, approve actions or change records without the approval the Service requires;
- use Q's output to build a competing AI model or dataset;
- present Q's output as human professional advice.

## 6. Protect the Service

You must not:

- scrape, crawl or bulk-download data from the Service, or access it by automated means except through features we provide for that purpose;
- probe, scan or test the Service's security without our written permission;
- upload malware or files designed to harm systems;
- overload or disrupt the Service, or get around rate limits, allowances or access controls;
- reverse engineer the Service, except where the law allows despite this restriction;
- resell or share access to your account.

If you find a security problem, please report it to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We welcome responsible reports.

## 7. What we do about breaches

If we reasonably believe you have broken this policy, we may, depending on how serious it is:

- warn you;
- remove or restrict content;
- limit features or allowances;
- suspend or close your account;
- report the matter to the authorities where required or appropriate.

Where it is lawful and safe, we will tell you what we did and why, and give you a chance to respond.

## 8. Reporting misuse

To report content or behaviour that breaks this policy, use the report option in the Service or write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.
