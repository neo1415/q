# Meeting Recording and Transcription Notice

> **In short**
>
> - Q joins a call only when a party to the meeting asks it to. Today Q joins Google Meet calls.
> - Q says who it is as soon as it joins, and its name in the call shows it is taking notes.
> - Q transcribes the call and writes notes for both sides. It can see shared screens only if the organiser allows it.
> - The organiser can remove Q or decline recording at any time. Anyone who does not want to be recorded can tell the organiser or leave the call.

## What Q says when it joins

Spoken aloud when Q arrives:

> I'm Q from {{COMPANY_NAME}}. I'll take notes for both sides and help when asked.

If the organiser lets Q see shared screens, Q adds:

> I can also see screens you share, for my own notes; never your cameras.

If the organiser also lets Q see cameras, Q adds instead:

> I can also see shared screens and cameras, for my own private notes.

## How Q appears in the call

Q joins with the name **Q ({{COMPANY_NAME}} notes)**, so everyone can see it is present and taking notes.

## Notice shown before the meeting

Shown in the invitation and on the meeting page:

> Q from {{COMPANY_NAME}} joins as "Q ({{COMPANY_NAME}} notes)" to keep the record of this call for both sides. It says so when it joins, and the organiser can end recording from {{COMPANY_NAME}}.

## What is recorded

- A transcript of what is said, with speaker names.
- Q's notes and summary, including any follow-ups it suggests.
- If allowed by the organiser, what Q sees on shared screens (and cameras, if allowed), used only for its notes.

## Who sees the record

The record belongs to the meeting's relationship. Both sides of that relationship can see the transcript and notes. It is not shown to anyone else unless one side chooses to share it.

## Your choices

- **Decline recording.** The organiser can decline recording, or remove Q, from the meeting page in {{COMPANY_NAME}}. Everyone on the call, and {{COMPANY_NAME}}, can see that recording was declined.
- **Remove Q from the call.** The meeting's host can remove Q using the meeting platform's own controls.
- **Ask in the call.** If you ask Q to leave during the call, Q records your request and passes it to the organiser. Because Q keeps the record for both sides, only the organiser can remove it.
- **Leave the meeting** if you do not want to be recorded.

## Q has no authority in the call

Nothing said in a meeting can make Q send messages, change records or take any action. Q says:

> I can't do that from the call. I'm here to take notes and help with what this meeting has shared; I'll put it to the organiser as a follow-up to approve afterwards.
