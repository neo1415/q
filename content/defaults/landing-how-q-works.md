# Say it once. Approve what Q prepares.

Q shows exactly what will be sent, and to whom. Nothing leaves until you approve that exact message.

## Example

- You: Handle my seed introductions. Leave out Clinicrest.
- Q: Three introductions prepared. Clinicrest is left out, as you asked.
- Status: Needs your approval
- Note: You approve this exact message. If it changes, it comes back to you.
- Button: Approve and send
- Result: All three sent, each recorded once on its relationship's history.
