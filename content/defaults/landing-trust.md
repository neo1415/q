# Built so you can trust what you see.

Q does the analysis. You make the decisions. {{COMPANY_NAME}} keeps the rules.

## Ranking can't be bought.

Investors see companies in an order set by mandate, fit, evidence and freshness.

| Counts                 | Never counts         |
| ---------------------- | -------------------- |
| Declared mandate       | Payment              |
| Fit                    | Views and watch time |
| Evidence and freshness | Who you know         |

## Evidence before opinion.

Every fact shows who said it and how well it's supported.

| Fact            | Support              | Source                                |
| --------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Monthly revenue | Document-supported   | Management accounts, Q3               |
| Customers       | Self-reported        | Founder, onboarding                   |
| Churn           | Unknown              | Not a black mark. Just not known yet. |
| Headcount       | Two figures disagree | Both shown. Neither picked.           |

## Founder-private data stays private.

What a founder keeps private never shapes what an investor is shown.

- Cash in bank: private
- Monthly burn: private
- Payroll: private. Filtered before Q or the ranking sees it.

## One shared record.

One relationship per company and investor. Its stage comes from what happened, never from a model.
