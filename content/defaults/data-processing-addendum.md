# Data Processing Addendum (outline)

> **In short**
>
> - When your organisation puts other people's personal data into {{COMPANY_NAME}}, you control that data and we process it for you.
> - We use it only on your instructions, keep it confidential and secure, and use approved sub-processors.
> - We help you meet data subject requests, tell you about breaches without undue delay, and delete or return the data when you leave.
> - Ask {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} for the signed version.

This addendum forms part of the agreement between the customer ("Customer") and {{LEGAL_ENTITY}} ("{{COMPANY_NAME}}") for the Service, when signed or accepted by the Customer.

## 1. Roles

| Data                                                                                                                              | Customer's role                                                 | Our role                                          |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Personal data the Customer's team puts into its own workspace (pipeline, notes, contacts, imported CRM files, internal documents) | Controller                                                      | Processor                                         |
| Account, billing, security and Service-operation data                                                                             | Not applicable                                                  | Controller (see [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy)) |
| Data a user shares with another organisation on the Service                                                                       | Each organisation is a separate controller for what it receives | Processor for each, as instructed                 |

## 2. Details of processing

| Item                  | Description                                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Subject matter        | Providing the Service under the agreement                                                                                                   |
| Duration              | The term of the agreement, plus the deletion period in section 9                                                                            |
| Nature and purpose    | Hosting, storage, analysis by Q, search, transcription, communication and sharing as the Customer instructs                                 |
| Data subjects         | The Customer's personnel; founders, investors, advisers and other contacts the Customer records                                             |
| Categories of data    | Contact and professional details; company and investment information; documents; messages; meeting transcripts and notes; voice transcripts |
| Special category data | Not intended. The Customer should not upload it unless necessary and lawful                                                                 |

## 3. Our obligations

We will:

- process personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions, including those given through the Service, unless the law requires otherwise (and then tell the Customer unless the law forbids it);
- ensure that people authorised to process the data are bound by confidentiality;
- implement the security measures in section 5;
- not use the Customer's personal data to train general-purpose AI models;
- assist the Customer, taking into account the nature of processing, with data subject requests, security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments and consultation with regulators;
- make available the information needed to show compliance with this addendum.

## 4. Sub-processors

- The Customer gives general authorisation for us to use sub-processors, including hosting, database, AI model, speech, video, meeting-bot, search, email, malware-scanning and support providers.
- We keep a current list and give at least 30 days' notice of a new sub-processor. The Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds. If we cannot resolve the objection, the Customer may end the affected Service and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees.
- We impose data protection terms on each sub-processor that are no less protective than this addendum, and remain responsible for their performance.

## 5. Security

Our measures include:

- encryption in transit and at rest;
- tenant isolation enforced in the database;
- role-based access within each organisation;
- two-step verification;
- malware scanning of uploads;
- logged and limited staff access;
- filtering of data by user permission before any AI processing;
- backups and recovery procedures.

We review these measures regularly and may update them provided protection is not reduced.

## 6. Breaches

We will notify the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the Customer's data, with the information reasonably available, and update it as we learn more. We will take reasonable steps to contain and remedy the breach.

## 7. International transfers

Where processing involves a restricted transfer, the parties agree to the applicable standard contractual clauses, the UK addendum, or the safeguards required under the NDPA, which are incorporated by reference.

## 8. Audits

We will answer reasonable written questions about our compliance and share summaries of relevant third-party audits or certifications where available. On-site audits are allowed once a year, on 30 days' notice, during business hours, at the Customer's cost, and subject to confidentiality, or more often where a regulator requires it.

## 9. Return and deletion

When the agreement ends, the Customer may export its data through the Service or on request for 30 days. After that we delete the Customer's personal data, except where the law requires us to keep it. Data in backups is overwritten on the normal cycle.

## 10. Liability and precedence

Each party's liability under this addendum is subject to the limits in the agreement, except where the law does not allow it. If this addendum conflicts with the agreement on data protection, this addendum prevails.
