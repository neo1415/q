# Three steps. Q does the reading.

## If you're investing

1. **State your mandate in a few minutes.** Stage, sector, geography, cheque size. Skip anything.
2. **See a feed ordered by fit and evidence.** Never by payment. The reasons sit on every pitch.
3. **Approve what Q prepares.** Introductions, follow-ups and passes, each one yours to send.

## If you're raising

1. **Upload what you already have.** A deck, accounts, a data pack. No questionnaire first.
2. **Q works first.** It shows what's known and how well supported, and asks only about the gaps.
3. **Approve what Q prepares.** Introductions to investors who fit, sent only after you approve them.
