# Where it leads.

Not months of outreach to investors who were never a fit. Not a message you never saw.

## If you're raising

A short list of investors who fit, each conversation on one shared record, every message sent only after you approved it.

## If you're investing

A pipeline that fits your mandate with the reasons visible, and one history both sides share.
