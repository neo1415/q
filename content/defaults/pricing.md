# Pricing

Usage allowances, nothing locked away.

Every plan can ask Q, bring Q to meetings, and see the full InvestIQ diagnosis. A plan sets how much is included each month. If you run out, buy a top-up or move up a plan.

## Always included, on every plan

- **Ask Q** by typing or by voice, in English and other languages, including Arabic, French, Spanish and Swahili.
- **The full InvestIQ diagnosis** of a company: strengths, gaps, risks and readiness, with the evidence behind each.
- **Q in your meetings.** Book calls through your Google calendar and Q joins Google Meet to take notes for both sides.
- **Charts and tables** in Q's answers when a picture says it better.
- **Your data, always yours.** Upload, see and remove your own information whatever plan you are on.
- **Security:** two-step verification and every upload scanned for malware.
- **No paid ranking.** No plan can buy a higher place in anyone's feed.

## Questions about limits

### What happens when I reach my allowance?

At 80% of any allowance, Q tells you once. At 100%, Q says so and offers a top-up or an upgrade. It never cuts off an answer halfway through.

### What if I run out of voice minutes during a conversation?

You get a two-minute warning, then the voice conversation ends politely. You can carry on by typing.

### Can I book a meeting with Q's video face beyond my allowance?

No. Q tells you when you try to book, not on the day, so you can top up first.

### Do unused allowances roll over?

No. Allowances reset each month. Top-ups carry over until used, or 12 months from purchase.

### Is anything ever cut off?

No. Your diagnosis and your own data stay available on every plan, and meetings with Q are never locked. When you reach your question allowance, Q tells you and offers a top-up or an upgrade. Safety limits apply to every account to keep the service fast and fair for everyone.

### Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time in your plan settings. You keep your paid allowance until the end of the period you paid for, then move to the free plan.
