# Landing page BrandScript (StoryBrand SB7)

The landing page at `/` for the 2026-10-05 demo. The customer is the hero;
Capital Q, through Q, is the guide. Every claim comes from the five in
`industry-comparison.md`; nothing here is a measured result.

## One-liner

**Private capital, on evidence.** Founders raising and investors deploying
lose months to noise. Q reads the evidence, prepares the next step, and acts
only when you approve, so the right relationships move forward.

## Hero 1: the founder raising

| SB7              | BrandScript                                                                                                                  |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Character wants  | To get in front of investors whose mandate actually fits, and be judged on what the company is.                              |
| External problem | Visibility follows reach and money: warm intros, paid listings, cold email nobody answers.                                   |
| Internal problem | "I don't know why I'm being ignored, or whether anyone read the deck."                                                       |
| Philosophical    | A good company should not need to buy its way to the top of a list.                                                          |
| Guide: empathy   | "You should be judged on evidence, not on who you know."                                                                     |
| Guide: authority | Ranking can't be bought; unknown stays unknown, never a black mark; founder-private data is firewalled.                      |
| Plan             | 1. Upload what you already have. 2. Q works first and shows what's known and how well supported. 3. Approve what Q prepares. |
| Direct CTA       | Get started (as a founder).                                                                                                  |
| Transitional CTA | Try GateQ: am I a fit? (a fictional demo investor, run in the browser).                                                      |
| Failure avoided  | Months of outreach to investors who were never a fit; private numbers leaking into someone else's judgement.                 |
| Success          | A short list of investors who fit, each conversation on one shared record, every message sent only after you approved it.    |

## Hero 2: the investor deploying

| SB7              | BrandScript                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Character wants  | To find companies that match the mandate, and spend time on evidence rather than inbox triage.                                                      |
| External problem | Decks as attachments, claims mixed with opinion, every tool keeping its own version of where things stand.                                          |
| Internal problem | "I can't tell what's verified, what's claimed, and what I'm missing."                                                                               |
| Philosophical    | A capital decision deserves evidence before opinion.                                                                                                |
| Guide: empathy   | "Your time belongs to the companies that fit."                                                                                                      |
| Guide: authority | Deterministic, explainable ranking; every fact shows who said it and how well it is supported; one shared record per company–investor relationship. |
| Plan             | 1. State your mandate in a few minutes. 2. See a feed ordered by fit and evidence, never by payment. 3. Approve what Q prepares.                    |
| Direct CTA       | Get started (as an investor).                                                                                                                       |
| Transitional CTA | See how Q works (the scripted "Watch Q work" illustration).                                                                                         |
| Failure avoided  | Missing a good company because it didn't have a warm intro; an assistant sending something you never saw.                                           |
| Success          | A mandate-fit pipeline with the reasons visible, and a relationship history that both sides read from the same row.                                 |

## Page order

Hero → Problem (both sides) → Guide (Q, five claims) → Plan (3 steps) →
Watch Q work (labelled illustration) → Try GateQ (demo investor, fictional) →
Success (founder, investor) → Failure avoided → Rehearsal teaser → FAQ →
Final CTA → Footer.

## Rules the copy keeps

- No stats, logos, testimonials or "X% faster". Illustrations are labelled.
- Never "verified identities", "fraud-proof", or capital raised.
- Success stories are written as what the product does for a person, not as
  quotes from people who do not exist.

## Restraint references

Linear (headline, one paragraph, a real product surface, no decorative
icons), Stripe (editorial whitespace, restrained colour), Anthropic (warm
editorial over dark tech). Colour carries information; motion only shows a
real state change.
