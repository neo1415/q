# Overnight 2026-10-05 → 06: master checklist

Every item from the founder's brief (5 Oct, evening), in his words where possible. Nothing here is optional. Each item has an id; the morning report answers every id with **done** (and how it was tested), **partial** (what is missing) or **not done** (why).

Process for every UI item: research → mockups (phone and desktop, light and dark, every state) → build exactly → test → screenshots of the real build. Mobile-first, fast, plain words. "Not a PDF-type design": a proper enterprise product.

The founder pre-approved shipping once the mockups and screenshots exist.
