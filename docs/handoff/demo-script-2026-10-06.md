# Capital Q demo script — 6 October 2026

Sign in as Zino (investor, Zino Aviation). Dark mode, black and gold. Laptop browser (Chrome) for the main flow; have the phone ready for the Explore and pitch-video moment. Keep sound on: Q's voice and soft sounds are part of the show.

Total: about 12 minutes. Every step below uses things that are live today. Steps marked *(if fixed)* depend on fixes landing before the demo; skip them if they don't look right in your rehearsal.

## 1. Open on Q (1 min)

Land on **Home**. The black-and-gold presence breathes in the middle of the screen.

Say: *"Capital Q isn't a database you search. It's an investment analyst that works for you. This is Q."*

Click **Talk with Q** and say: **"What's in my feed today that fits my mandate?"**

What they see: Q answers out loud and the companies come up as cards ranked against Zino's mandate (stage, sector, geography). The card Q is talking about grows while the others shrink.

## 2. Top three side by side (2 min)

Say to Q: **"Give me the top three, side by side."**

What they see: three coloured cards with a score out of 10 next to plain words ("7.5/10 · Good fit"), three reasons each, and the measures behind it. Q walks through them in voice.

Point out: *"No black-box percentages. The score is computed from things you can check, and anything unknown never counts against a founder."*

## 3. A founder's pitch (2 min)

Click into **Clearwater Assurance** (or Ledgerline).

- **Elevator** tab: play the founder's vertical pitch video, with captions and the founder's name.
- **Overview**: "Fit with your mandate" panel on the right, with each parameter, and **Q's view** labelled clearly as Q's opinion, not a verified fact.
- **Pitch deck**: Q has read the deck into the same twelve sections for every founder; swipe or use the arrows.
- **Data room**: per-document visibility; sensitive documents stay hidden until shared.
- **Team**: founders and team.

Say: *"Every founder lands in the same structure, so you compare like with like, and nothing private leaks to you unless the founder shares it."*

## 4. Ask Q about the company (1 min)

Open Q and say: **"Give me a first reading of Clearwater: what stands out, what needs attention, and what you still don't know."**

What they see: an analyst's reading that separates what's known from what isn't.

## 5. Q searches the web (1 min)

Say: **"Search the web for recent news on model risk regulation for UK banks."**

What they see: Q answers from real web pages and lists its sources underneath. *(New today; rehearse it once before the demo.)*

## 6. Q works while you sleep (2 min)

Go to **Work**. Show the standing instruction: *"As soon as any company matches my mandate, express interest."*

Say: *"When a new founder is verified and matches, Q expresses interest for me within about a minute and a half. Anything that speaks for me waits for my approval."* Show an approval card and approve one.

Then ask Q: **"What are you working on?"** *(if fixed: the approval cards appear right in the answer.)*

## 7. Explore (1 min, on the phone)

Open **Explore**: a masonry grid of every pitch on the network. Tap one; scroll up to see related pitches, TikTok-style, with a back button. *(if fixed: the improved masonry.)*

## 8. GateQ (1 min)

Open **GateQ**. Show the inbox: every founder who came through Zino's gate, with stars, labels, notes and a one-click download pack. *"It's Gmail for your deal flow, with Q drafting the replies for you to approve."*

## 9. Close on voice (30 s)

Say: **"Hey Q, take me to Discover."** *(Hey Q needs the toggle on in Settings → Q, and Chrome.)* Or just tap the mic and say it.

Close: *"Evidence before opinion, the investor stays in control, and Q does the work in between."*

## Before you start (5 minutes)

- Rehearse step 5 (web search) and step 6 once.
- Turn on Settings → Q → "Say 'Hey Q' to start" if you want the wake word; allow the microphone.
- If anything misbehaves live, say "let me show you on the profile instead" and go to step 3. It's the most reliable part.
