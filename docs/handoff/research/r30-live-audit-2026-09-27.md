Scope: the deployed stack (web `capital-qweb-production`, api, q-api on Railway), audited 06:35–07:15 UTC 2026-09-27.

- Accounts: seeded fictional accounts only (`investor.lagoon-angels`, `founder.ledgerfold`).
- Viewports: 1440×900 and 390×844, in light and dark.
- Tools: headless Chromium through Playwright, plus curl. The Chromium was pinned to trust only the session proxy CA.
- Q turns used: 6 typed, short. No voice.

Severity key: **P0** = broken, **P1** = wrong, **P2** = polish.

Totals: **P0 1 · P1 10 · P2 25.**

Side effects on fictional data:

- **Ledgerfold:** the headline was changed through an approval. A Q Card now exists at `/@ledgerfold`.
- **Lagoon Angels:** it expressed interest in Kazikit and saved Kazikit and Tarmacly. Q made a mandate PDF.

Environment limit: this Chromium build has no H.264 (`MediaSource.isTypeSupported('video/mp4; codecs="avc1…"') === false`). The audit could not see real pitch playback; see finding 22.

## Findings

### P0

1. **P0: "Continue with Google" sign-in fails with Google `redirect_uri_mismatch`.**
   - Page: `/auth/sign-in` and `/auth/sign-up`.
   - Repro: signed out, click "Continue with Google".
   - Evidence: the app lands on `accounts.google.com/signin/oauth/error?authError=…`. The decoded error is `redirect_uri_mismatch … redirect_uri https://vcohxiqsmnkzxnvawgri.supabase.co/auth/v1/callback`.
   - Cause: configuration, not code. The Supabase auth callback is not an authorised redirect URI on the Google OAuth client that Supabase's Google provider uses. It is a different flow from the Gmail connect, which works (see "Checked and working").
   - Code: `apps/web/src/features/auth/components/google-sign-in.tsx` and `signInWithGoogleAction` in `apps/web/src/auth/actions`.
   - Fix: add the callback in the Google Cloud Console, or hide the button until it is configured.

### P1

2. **P1: the Q dock is stuck at the top-left corner (0,0) on desktop and covers the "Capital Q" wordmark.**
   - Pages: every app page at 1440 (`/discover`, `/relationships`, `/profile`, `/capital`).
   - Repro: load any page at 1440×900 and wait 6 s.
   - Evidence: `.cq-q-dock-layer button` has its rect at `0,0 44x44`. The wrapper has inline `style="transform: none"`. Screenshot: `audit-shots/probe-investor.lagoon-angels-_relationships-1440-light.png`.
   - At 390 wide the dock sits bottom-right correctly. An earlier load in the same session placed it bottom-right, so it looks like a race or a regression in a recent deploy.
   - Likely cause: `apps/web/src/features/q-dock/q-dock.tsx`. The `useLayoutEffect` at ~207–233 writes `box.style.transform`, and the `m.div style={{ x, y }}` motion values (still 0) overwrite it.

3. **P1: the "Sources" disclosure shows a missing-glyph box followed by "B8".**
   - Page: `/profile` and every other place that uses `.cq-sources-summary`.
   - Evidence: the text renders as "▯B8 Sources". In `apps/web/app/globals.css:458`, `content: "^UB8";` holds a literal U+0015 control character plus "B8". It should be the escape `"\25B8"` (▸); the backslash escape was mangled into a control byte.

4. **P1: the Q Card public page's `og:image` points at `http://localhost:3000`.**
   - Page: `/@ledgerfold`, signed in or out.
   - Evidence: `<meta property="og:image" content="http://localhost:3000/u/ledgerfold/opengraph-image?beb77daf00beb549">`. The canonical link is correct. Every share preview (WhatsApp, LinkedIn, iMessage) will be broken.
   - Cause: no `metadataBase` anywhere in `apps/web` (`grep -rn metadataBase apps/web` returns nothing). Set it in `apps/web/app/layout.tsx` from the public origin.

5. **P1: the public Q Card presents synthetic demo attestation as real verification.**
   - Page: `/@ledgerfold`, signed out.
   - Evidence: the page says "Organisation verified by Capital Q" and "Founder identity verified by Capital Q". The owner's `/profile` says "Verified (synthetic demo attestation). This is demo data on a demo deployment, not a real-world check." The public, external page drops that qualifier.
   - Code: `apps/web/src/features/q-card/public-card-view.tsx:33-34`. It needs the same attestation-kind distinction that `profile-enrichment.tsx` uses. This is an evidence-honesty issue on a `public_external` surface.

6. **P1: typed approval is impossible, even though Q says "or tell me to go ahead".**
   - Page: `/home`, founder.
   - Repro: type "Change my headline to: …". Q replies "Not saved yet: tap Approve on the card, or tell me to go ahead."

   - This is founder-fixture failure #1 on the typed path.
   - Code: `apps/web/src/features/q/q-conversation.tsx:981`, `disabled={q.working && !voice.active}`. A run waiting on approval counts as "working".
   - Related: the Now card says "Q is working" together with "Waiting for your approval", and there are two Stop buttons (`apps/web/src/features/q/q-now.tsx:61`).

7. **P1: Discover "Saved" does not survive a reload, and there is no Saved list.**

   - Repro: Save Tarmacly. The button shows "Saved" with `aria-pressed=true`. Reload and page to Tarmacly: it shows "Save" with `aria-pressed=false`.
   - Cause: decisions live only in client reducer state (`apps/web/src/features/discover/feed/feed-state.ts:189`, `decisions: {}`). The slate DTO carries no viewer decision. With no Saved page (ux-direction §13, "Saved: Missing"), a save is invisible after reload.

8. **P1: the mandate PDF and viewer show raw codes, mangled labels and a false gap.**

- Home briefing (founder: "One thing needs you", the Savanna connection).

- Approve on the card saves, and the change shows on the profile.
- Mandate PDF: the Board card, the viewer modal (Esc closes it) and the download.
- Discover: 9 cards at desktop, full-height player, Next/Previous. Save and Express interest are server-confirmed ("Interest expressed in Kazikit."), and the company then appears in `/relationships` and `/capital`.
- Relationship detail pages for both sides.
- Chrome theme dropdown (Light/Dark/System) and the Settings theme sync.
- Gmail connect goes to `accounts.google.com` with `redirect_uri=https://capital-qapi-production.up.railway.app/v1/integrations/google/callback`, the correct production host. `?google=failed` shows a clear message.

- Unknown company and relationship IDs show a calm "isn't available to you".
- API problem details (`application/problem+json`, 401).
- Across all 4 configs on every app page: no horizontal overflow at 390, and no "undefined", "null" or NaN text. Nothing in any page's HTML points at localhost except the `og:image` in finding 4. No 5xx.
