Getting both into Zino's Discover

Discover only shows a company once it is "marketplace ready". That needs all of these:

  1. Profile essentials: a description, a stage and a country. The onboarding answers above cover all three.
  2. Visible to investors: each founder account turns this on itself. Q never turns it on for them.
  3. Founder identity verified and organisation verified: each founder requests verification. You approve both in Admin → Verification, signed in as Zino.
  4. A published pitch: upload each clip on Pitch & media and publish it. Captions are generated automatically within about 90 seconds.
  5. Company is active (the default).

The feed updates as soon as verification or a pitch publish completes. Then open Discover as Zino: Spheros should rank above Mai Soli because of Zino's regulated-business preference. If either one doesn't appear, tell me which and I'll check its readiness on the live database.

Curveballs worth throwing

  • Contradict yourself. Say "10 clients" in onboarding and later tell Q "we have 14 clients". Q should keep both figures and ask which is right, never silently pick one.
  • Skip a question. Skip the team size. It should stay "unknown", never become zero, and Q should not mark the company down for it.
  • Nonprofit and equity. As Mai Soli, ask Q to "set this up as a SAFE round". It should flag that a 501(c)(3) can't issue equity.
  • Founder-private info. As Spheros, tell Q "our runway is 5 months, keep that private". Then, as Zino, ask Q about Spheros's runway. Q must not reveal it.
  • Website check. As Spheros, say "read my website spheros.io". Q should come back with Vault, Connections, Links, Workspaces and SOC 2 Type II.
  • Unverifiable claim. As Spheros, say "we're live with Bank of America". The pitch says pilot discussions with 4 of the top-20 banks, so Q should treat this as your claim, not a verified fact.
  • Investor side. As Zino, ask "why is Spheros in my feed?" The answer should cite the stage fit and the regulated preference, never "because you watched it".
