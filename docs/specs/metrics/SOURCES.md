# Sources for the metrics methods

These are the sources cited in [METRICS-METHODS.md](METRICS-METHODS.md). They were gathered on 7 Nov 2026 by three read-only research passes. The earlier InvestIQ v1 basis (R1–R12: Payne scorecard, Berkus and the rest) is in `docs/product-sources/investiq/RESEARCH.md`, and still applies where it is not superseded here.

**Verification level of each source.**

- **[V]:** the primary text or the full PDF was read.
- **[A]:** only the abstract or the publisher record was read.
- **[S]:** seen only in a secondary summary or a search snippet. **Do not quote an [S] number in product copy until a person has opened the source.**

The sources inform the direction and relative size of the weights. None of them fits these exact weights.

## Machine learning on startup databases (no usable weights)

S19 covers three studies:

- Żbikowski & Antosiuk (2021), _Information Processing & Management_ 58(4):102555.
- Arroyo et al. (2019), _IEEE Access_ 7:124233. [doi:10.1109/ACCESS.2019.2938659](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2019.2938659)
- Potanin et al. (2023), arXiv:2309.15552.

Their feature importances could not be verified. Their strongest predictors are financing-history features, which partly leak the outcome being predicted.

**Not to be cited** until they are checked:

- Azoulay's "125%" figure;
- the exact counts in the CB Insights funnel;
- the 30–50% survival effect in Kerr et al.;
- the return multiples in Wiltbank & Boeker;
- Carta's finding that bridge rounds are 10× less likely to lead to a next round.
