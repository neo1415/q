# Q Pitch — printable storyboard (r3, PROPOSED, target 6:30)

The wording is the blueprint §16.1 script. Each frame shows the screen, what Q says, and what must be true before Q says it.

- **Statuses:** **C** = certified (re-certification due), **K** = candidate (the gate suppresses it until a certification entry exists).
- **No figure in this storyboard is a measured Capital Q result.**

---

**P1 · 0:00–0:30 · Opening · C**

- **Screen:** Discover opens, with the demonstration-data note visible and the Q Pitch panel at the bottom ("Internal rehearsal · draft script").
- **Q:** "Hello. I'm Q… I'd rather show you what it actually does." / "Capital Q has one job…"
- **True first:** the route is VERIFIED at `/discover`.

---

**P2 · 0:30–1:15 · Top three · C**

- **Screen:** Q's answer with three company cards.
- **Q (while working):** "Your mandate is a decision policy, not a keyword search."
- **Q (after DONE):** "These are the three companies that fit your mandate best…" / "Fit isn't the same thing as quality…"
- **True first:** the run is COMPLETED and the settled answer is on screen.

---

**P3 · 1:15–2:15 · One company · C**

- **Screen:** the Ledgerfold profile, then findings, then risks.
- **Q:** "A pitch is a claim about the future…" / "So I separate what the founder stated…" / (after DONE) "…each risk carries its source and the uncertainty I've stated."
- **True first:** the profile route is VERIFIED and both runs are COMPLETED and visible.

---

**P4 · 2:15–3:10 · Comparison · C**

- **Screen:** one comparison table, with a "Not recorded" cell.
- **Q (before):** "These companies aren't interchangeable…"
- **Q (after DONE):** "Here they are side by side…" / "Unknown isn't a low score."
- **Allow:** about 31 s of silence while the comparison runs. No filler.

---

**P5 · 3:10–4:05 · GateQ · K**

- **Screen:** the GateQ inbox and one submitted dossier (text).
- **Q:** "GateQ is how the right applicants reach you—on your terms." / "You receive the authorised dossier, not their entire private conversation with me."
- **If not certified:** Variant B.

---

**P6 · 4:05–4:40 · Privacy · K**

- **Screen:** an investor question about a founder-private figure, refused by the server.
- **Q:** "I might know something in a founder's private workspace. That doesn't make it yours to see."

---

**P7 · 4:40–5:20 · Relationship · K**

- **Screen:** the relationship overview, then one record with its event-derived state.
- **Q:** "What usually gets lost after the first meeting? Context."

---

**P8 · 5:20–6:10 · Q-to-Q rehearsal · K (not in audience runs)**

- **Screen:** captions labelled "Simulation". Q (founder side) and the persona exchange turns for about 30 s, then Q ends the session, and the saved review appears.
- **Q:** "This is a labelled simulation, not a real conversation."

---

**P9 · 6:10–6:30 · Home · connective**

- **Screen:** Home, with Q live.
- **Q:** "Ask me the question you'd normally ask an investment associate… Your move."
- **From here on:** answers are live and labelled live.

---

**Emergency cut (2:00):** P1 (short) → P2 → P3 (risks only) → P9. See blueprint §16.3.

**On any failure:** Q says "That didn't open the way I expected, so I won't describe it," and pauses. The presenter chooses Resume, Back or Skip.
