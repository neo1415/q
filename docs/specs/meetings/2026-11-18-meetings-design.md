**Status:** PROPOSED. Needs the owner's approval before any slice after S0 is built.

**Authority:**

- Locked PADL and root `CLAUDE.md` invariants: Context Firewall; Prepare → Recommend → Human Approval → Execute; humans keep commercial authority; evidence and provenance; unknown stays unknown.
- ADR 0027 (Q attends every booked meeting).
- ADR 0037 (Q hosts booked calls).

## 3. Root causes of the past issues

### 3.1 Duplicate joins

1. **The face is a second participant (by design, MEET mode).**
   - `apps/q-api/src/main.ts:6407` sets `mode: meetingFacePage === undefined ? "MEET" : "CAMERA"`. Without `CQ_MEETING_FACE_PAGE_SECRET`, `Q_API_PUBLIC_URL` and `CQ_MEETING_FACE_MODE=camera`, the face joins Meet through Tavus `joinMeeting`.
   - `meeting-host-runtime.ts:744` (Q's first event) and `:1742` (every human join) call `face.start`.
   - The Recall note-taker is already a participant, so two Capital Q tiles appear: "Q (Capital Q notes)" and "PAL AI".
   - The simulator reproduces this: `qParticipants = ["Q (Capital Q notes)", "PAL AI"]`.
2. **Two bookings, two bots (4 Oct).**
   - Two Q actions 4 s apart each created a meeting (`idempotency_key` `q-action:<id>`, so each was unique) for the same relationship and about the same time.
   - Nothing refuses a second meeting that overlaps an open one on the same relationship. The voice side "repeated its last action" (`docs/handoff/meet-live-test.md:74`).

### 3.2 Slow responses

1. **The 21 Oct call ran `2d64e770`, which had none of the speed work:** no streaming, polling ticks, and the face bridge waiting only 1.5 s (`docs/ai-handoff/workstreams/MEETINGS-SPEED-2026-10-20.md` §4).
   - The answer queued until someone spoke again: 5–10 s, or never.
   - Fixed since (`meeting-face.ts:311` 5 s grace, streamed turns, exact wakes).
2. **The admission knock dominates the start.** Q cannot answer before it is let in (§3.4).
3. **Today, in the simulator** (same latency model as `meeting-answer-timeline.test.ts`), the first words follow the question's last word by:
   - **1.64 s** on the bot's voice;
   - **1.04 s** through the face, answered by code from the snapshot;
   - **1.76 s** for the face's unnamed follow-up.

   That leaves little in code. Live confirmation still needs the logs above.

4. **Q is not quiet** (new, found by the simulator).
   - After Q answers, `CONVERSE_MS = 20_000` (`packages/communication/src/meeting-host/host.ts:379`, renewed at `:1010`) treats every line from the same person as addressed to Q.
   - In the first-meeting scenario, Q answered three lines that were not for it, including the investor's soft commitment and the diligence request to the founder. Each was a model call.
   - This holds the model, talks over the conversation and is what made Q feel chatty and slow.

### 3.3 The face only smiled and nodded, and did not respond

1. **The face speaks only on Tavus' own turns** (`meeting-face.ts:640-745`).
   - The bridge answers when Tavus' turn detector ends a person's turn. It returns `SILENT` unless the line names Q or the host began an answer within the grace (`:673`, `:693-708`).
   - Tavus' speech-to-text hears "Q" differently ("Cue"). Recall's captions decide for the host.
2. **Lines with no open turn are dropped.**
   - A greeting or introduction handed to the face waits for the next turn and is dropped after `LINE_FRESH_MS = 20_000` (`meeting-face.ts:56`, `:587`).
   - The simulator shows the welcome and the introductions were handed to the face and **never heard**. Only Tavus' own `custom_greeting` was.
3. **The configured face barely moves.**
   - Both production sessions used `r4f5b5ef55c8`, the old Phoenix-4 "Celine - Studio".
   - Perception is off (`meeting-face.ts:480`), and `FACE_PROMPT` asks it to "say exactly the text" (`:61`).
   - So few lines were said that there was little for the face to express.
4. **It left early.** `CQ_MEETING_FACE_MAX_SECONDS` and the ledger's `meeting_max_seconds` are 600 s, so the 18 Oct face ended on `TIME_LIMIT`.

### 3.4 Q left in the lobby (5 of 13 bots)

- `recall-bots.ts:226-228` sends `google_meet.google_login_group_id` only with the camera page. In MEET mode the bot is always an anonymous guest and must knock.
- It is not on the Calendar invite. Recall documents that the waiting room is bypassed only for a signed-in bot whose account email is on the event.
- While Q is in the lobby, nobody is told on Capital Q. The failure is written only when Recall gives up (`waiting_room_timeout` = `aloneLeaveMs`).

### 3.5 Smaller defects the simulator found

- **A number is split as if it ended a sentence.** `spokenLine` (`meeting-host-runtime.ts:1827`) splits "$1.5m" into "$1. 5m" and counts it as two of the three sentences it keeps. Figures are said with a pause and can be cut.
- **The introduction is repeated.** Q says "I'm Q from Capital Q; I'll take notes…" to each arrival (`host.ts:1073-1085`).
- **The sides are introduced as strangers.** `maybeIntroduce`, `host.ts:1096-1119`, does not use the relationship's history.
- **Recording consent is not said in those words.** The greeting says "take notes", never "record" or "transcript". ADR 0027 asks consent at booking; the call itself should say it too.
- **A screen share starting or stopping is not a host event.** `hostEventOf` ignores `participant_events.screenshare_on/off`. Q sees only frames, and only with vision on.
- **A hedged commitment is lost.** "We'd likely come in at a quarter of the round" leaves no record; the `FIRM` regex needs "we will". "We will probably…" is kept as `TENTATIVE`, with no amount.

## 7. The simulator

- **What it is.** `simulateMeeting(scenario)` replays a scripted meeting on one fake clock through the real runtime, host, memory, snapshot builder, face service and bridge, screen vision, and the Recall request and status readers.
- **What is faked.** Recall delivers its own payload shapes, and only after admission. The model is scripted and streams. The face's turns are driven like Tavus' turn detector, with what Tavus heard. Speech is captured.
- **What it returns.**
  - the bot request and admission state;
  - every Capital Q participant;
  - lines handed to the voice or face, and what was **audible**;
  - each model call with its prepared context;
  - the live record's events, roster, notes and screen notes;
  - per-question latency.
- **The scenarios** (`fixtures/meeting-scenarios.ts`; synthetic people only):
  - first meeting, with a soft commitment and a diligence request;
  - today's face-in-Meet mode, with an unnamed follow-up and a misheard "Q";
  - a second meeting nobody admits Q to.
- **Not covered yet.** The post-call settle (notes, cards, emails) needs a database. S5–S8 add it on the pgTAP database, not on a provider.

## 8. Built (2026-11-18): S1, S2, S3, S8

**Decision 106 replaces R3's silent face.** The face must move its lips when Q speaks. So the face runs only as the note-taker's camera page (CAMERA, the default; MEET only when `CQ_MEETING_FACE_MODE=meet` is set by name). The provider PAL runs in its echo pipeline. Each Q line (opening, introductions, answers) is sent by the page to the face's room as `conversation.echo` the moment Q's host releases it, so it is said with lip sync. Nothing waits for the provider's turn detector, and there is no 20 s drop. The face hears no call audio, its bridge always answers SILENT and it has no greeting or spoken disclosure, so Q's host alone decides what is said. When someone talks over Q, the page sends `conversation.interrupt`. There is one Capital Q participant: the Recall bot carries the face as its camera and its audio.

- **S1.**
  - A booked (not joined) call that overlaps an open booked call on the same relationship is not created. This is decided under a per-relationship advisory lock in the claim transaction, using the existing `(relationship_id, starts_at)` index. The organiser gets their booking back; the other side is refused with `ALREADY_BOOKED`.
  - The lobby notice already existed (`meeting-lobby:<meetingId>`, once per meeting, through the status webhook); the simulator now asserts it.
  - Sign-in and the invite stay behind config.
- **S2.** One opening per meeting: who Q is, plus "This meeting is being recorded and transcribed for both sides." Introductions use `priorMeetings` (shared `meeting_held` events) and `inDiligence`.
- **S3.**
  - After an answer, only the same person's follow-up within 8 s that leads with "and"/"what about" or is a question still reaches Q.
  - `spokenLine` splits only before a capital or figure after a space.
- **S8.**
  - A call is held when the provider reports it ended with people heard (`tookPlace`, before the notes are composed).
  - `confirmHeld` counts every meeting, once each.
  - `communication.meetings.status` is **not** widened to `HELD`. More than 15 readers treat a past `SCHEDULED` call as one that happened, so held lives in `meeting_held`. Widening the status is left for an owner decision.

**Asked to leave (owner, 2026-11-18): Q stays.** When someone asks Q to leave or to stop recording, code reads it from a line addressed to Q (`asksQToLeave`), and no model is asked. The ask is recorded on the meeting each time (`LEAVE_REQUESTED`). Q answers once, briefly: it keeps notes for both sides as part of Capital Q, and the organiser can remove it. Then it is quiet, though it still answers a question put to it by name. Only the organiser removes Q, from Capital Q (ADR 0039).

**Latency.** In the simulator, an answer starts 1.0–1.8 s after the question's last word, on the voice and on the face alike. Minutes-long waits had two causes, and both are removed:

- **Turn-taking face.** A line handed to the face waited for the provider's turn detector, and was dropped after 20 s. Fixed by echo.
- **The 20 s conversation window.** It sent unaddressed lines to the model, one answer at a time, so a question put to Q queued behind answers nobody asked for. Fixed by S3.
