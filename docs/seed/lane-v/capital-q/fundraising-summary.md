# What Capital Q is

Capital Q is an AI-native Investment Intelligence Operating System for private capital. It is not a fundraising marketplace, investor directory, CRM, pitch deck repository or document management platform. (Product Architecture Decision Log, Decision #001.)

Its primary purpose is to generate trusted investment intelligence that enables better investment decisions while reducing information asymmetry between founders and investors. (Product Architecture Decision Log, Decision #001.)

Q is Capital Q's investment intelligence analyst. Q learns each investor's thesis and conversationally onboards businesses to understand their traction, financials, team, raise and investment story. (Product Architecture Decision Log, Decisions #009, #010, #012; Capital Q landing page.)

Regardless of how a founder originally presents a company, GateQ converts the information into the same core structure, so investment teams can compare companies like for like. (GateQ Product Feature Specification, Standardised Application Profile.)

Capital Q is built by FDN Capital Partners, with offices in Lagos, Nigeria and Dubai, UAE. (Capital Q landing page, footer.)
