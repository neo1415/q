A4 · solution

Tell it the story. Get two finished songs in about a minute.

01 brief
Story, not a prompt box

An eight-step guided form, or the conversational AI Producer by chat or voice. Names, memories, occasion, genre, mood.

02 generate
30–60 seconds

Complete song: lyrics, vocals and music. Each generation returns two variations. AI-written lyrics, your own, or instrumental.

03 refine
Revise freely

Change genre, mood or lyrics. The AI Producer shows the credit cost and waits for confirmation before spending.

04 license
Download with rights

MP3 and WAV (lossless on higher tiers), a PDF licence certificate per paid order, a library kept “indefinitely”.

Self-reported All steps as described on songscribe.ai and in the FAQ answers in the site bundle (fetched 2026-10-18). Not independently tested for this deck.

The moat is not the model. It is the brief, the licence and the market.
