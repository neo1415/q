## 2. Description

SongScribe.AI generates complete songs (music, lyrics and vocals) from a guided brief. The brief is either an eight-step form or the conversational **AI Producer**, which takes chat or voice.

- **Generation time:** generation "typically takes 30-60 seconds". Each generation returns **2 variations**.
- **Lyrics:** users can let the AI write the lyrics, supply their own, or choose instrumental.
- **Downloads:** MP3 and WAV, with "higher tiers offering lossless WAV".
- **Licence certificate:** each paid order is described as carrying "a downloadable license certificate (PDF)".

All of the above is from the FAQ answers in the site bundle.

The founder's site describes SongScribe as building "a future where creating high quality, studio-grade music becomes as simple and universal as sending a message" ([zinomario.com](https://www.zinomario.com/)).

## 7. Business model (SELF_REPORTED, with inconsistencies flagged)

- **Model:** freemium credits subscription, plus credit top-ups and custom agency and enterprise licensing. The licence tier rises with plan price: personal, then commercial, then exclusive, then full rights for a team.
- **Free tier:** gated by preview only (60-second previews, no downloads).
- **Payments:** Stripe, Flutterwave, Payoneer and Seerbit.

**Pricing inconsistencies across the live site.** Material for diligence:

1. **[/pricing](https://www.songscribe.ai/pricing)** shows **monthly** plans: Free, Starter, Creator, Pro and Studio (table below).
2. **The FAQ** says: "Most plans are one-time purchases. Only the Studio Pro tier (**$199/mo**) is a subscription." It also says "The Studio Pro subscription offers unlimited variations."
3. **[/terms](https://www.songscribe.ai/terms)** and **[/licensing](https://www.songscribe.ai/licensing)** use different tier names: Personal, Standard, Business, Exclusive and "Studio Pro (Subscription)", which offers "Unlimited generation".
4. **[/refund](https://www.songscribe.ai/refund)** says "all purchases are final and non-refundable" and mentions add-ons such as "express delivery". The FAQ says that if delivery fails, "we'll make it right".
5. **Credit cost per generation is not published.** The free demo is described two ways: "10 AI variations… Resets Daily" and "3,000 credits to start".

**Reading (ASSUMPTION):** the company appears to have moved from one-time per-song tiers (the Nov 2025 terms) to monthly credit plans, without updating the FAQ, terms or licensing copy.

## 8. Capabilities and differentiators (SELF_REPORTED)

**Capabilities**

- Full-song generation: lyrics, vocals and music.
- Two variations per generation, plus revisions (change genre, mood or lyrics).
- An instrumental option.
- **AI Producer:** a conversational brief by chat or voice. It "says so" that it is software. It shows the cost and waits for confirmation before spending credits.
- An eight-step guided form.
- Multi-language lyrics ("Quality may vary by language").
- MP3 and WAV downloads, a PDF licence certificate, and an account library stored "indefinitely".

**Genres shown:** acoustic, hip-hop/trap, R&B, Afrobeat, Amapiano, gospel, EDM house, beats and instrumentals.

**Claimed differentiators**

- **Story-first personalisation** using names, memories and occasions, rather than a prompt box.
- **African genre and language fluency:** Yoruba and pidgin blending, Afrobeat and Amapiano.
- **Plan-based licensing** with an "Official Ownership Certificate" at the exclusive tier.

**Not disclosed:** the underlying generation model or vendor. This matters for licence chain-of-title (see Risks).

## 11. Licensing and terms (SELF_REPORTED, from [/licensing](https://www.songscribe.ai/licensing) and [/terms](https://www.songscribe.ai/terms))

**Personal & Standard**

- Allowed: download, share with friends and family, non-monetised social posts.
- Not allowed: ads, monetisation, brand use, resale or Content ID.

**Business (non-exclusive commercial)**

- Allowed: ads, monetised YouTube and podcasts, client projects.
- Not allowed: claiming exclusivity, Content ID registration, resale, or AI retraining.

**Exclusive**

- "Your song will NOT be resold".
- Use "globally, forever, in any media", including broadcast TV, cinema and radio.
- Content ID registration and an "Official Ownership Certificate".
- Still not allowed: resale as stock, claiming authorship, or AI retraining without permission.

**Ownership**

- "SongScribe retains the underlying AI generation technology rights, but the output usage is granted to you."
- The FAQ adds: "You do!" [own the song], with rights per tier.

**Other terms**

- **Content rules:** no copyrighted lyrics or melodies, no artist or celebrity sound-alikes or impersonation, and no sexual, hate or illegal content.
- **Age:** users must be at least 13; under-18s need guardian permission.
- **Data:** prompts are collected, and anonymised data may be used to improve AI systems. Personal data is not sold.
- **Liability:** "as-is" warranty disclaimer; "sole remedy is to discontinue use".
- **Indemnity:** users indemnify SongScribe, including for "Commercial use of generated content".
- **Governing law:** Nigeria.

## 16. Risks

1. **Chain of title and model provenance.** The generation engine is undisclosed. Exclusive and "full rights" grants are only as good as the upstream model's training-data legitimacy and its output terms. The major labels sued Suno and Udio; settlements arrived only in late 2025, and some suits (e.g. Sony, GEMA, Koda against Suno) were reported as still open ([Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2025/11/25/business/warner-music-lawsuit-ai-suno/)).
2. **Copyrightability.** Purely AI-generated music may not attract copyright in the US, where human authorship is required. "Ownership certificates" and Content ID registration for AI outputs can trigger platform disputes. Under UK CDPA s9(3), computer-generated works are treated differently. Under the Nigerian Copyright Act 2022, the position on AI authorship is untested (ASSUMPTION; needs legal opinion).
3. **Inconsistent public pricing and terms.** There are four conflicting tier schemes, and $179 vs $199. This exposes the company to consumer-protection complaints and chargebacks. Its strict no-refund stance adds to that.
4. **Entity mismatch.** The Terms name "SongScribe Ltd" under Nigerian law, but the verified entity is the English "SONGSCRIBE AI LTD", incorporated after the Terms' effective date.
5. **Commoditisation.** Suno and Udio set consumer price expectations, and they could add gifting flows.
6. **Founder bandwidth** across Zino Aviation, Kuuza, FDN and the Man-Up Initiative. The cofounder's role is unclear.
7. **Minors and personal data.** The service allows users from age 13, and the songs contain real names and personal stories.

## 17. Regulatory considerations

- **Nigeria:**
  - the NDPA 2023 governs personal data;
  - the Copyright Act 2022 and the Nigerian Copyright Commission cover registration and ownership;
  - the FCCPC Act 2018 covers consumer refunds and terms fairness.
- **UK** (where the entity is incorporated):
  - UK GDPR and the DPA 2018;
  - the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, under which digital-content cancellation rights need express consent and an acknowledgement to waive them;
  - the CDPA 1988, s9(3) on computer-generated works.
- **EU AI Act, Article 50:** AI-generated audio must be marked as such in a machine-readable way. The Act's general transparency obligations apply from 2 August 2026, so EU distribution needs output watermarking or metadata.
- **US:** the Copyright Office's AI guidance (2023, and the 2025 Part 2 report) requires human authorship. Right-of-publicity and voice-likeness statutes also apply, e.g. Tennessee's ELVIS Act 2024. The terms' ban on sound-alikes helps but is not by itself enough.
- **Platforms:** YouTube Content ID and streaming distributors have their own AI-content policies, which matter for "Register with Content ID" claims.
