Key decisions

  • Answers are cards with an identity hue, a score out of 10, three reasons and six measures in words (Strong, Good, Partial, Unknown) with distinct shapes, so colour never carries meaning alone. Unknown stays Unknown.
  • The card Q is talking about expands; the rest shrink to rows (up to three) or half-width tiles (four or more on a phone; a 1 + 3 × 3 grid for ten on desktop). Q’s presence moves to a 52 px mark beside the spoken line.
  • Every card and every answer has a close. A new topic sends the whole answer to the Board button, the count ticks up and the presence returns to the centre.
  • Suggestions run on one tap: the chip becomes the request line and Q starts at once. A PDF is only an item in the More menu, or a file you asked for.
  • The Board is a timeline of what Q showed, with Pinned and Files beside it (tabs on a phone). Sources fold under “Where this came from” with the evidence in plain words.
  • Q’s team reads like a run log: one job, its steps, who owns each, hand-offs, the reviewer’s score against a stated bar, and an approval bound to the exact text. Cost by specialist, with a monthly limit.
  • Motion: on-screen moves use cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1) at 420–560 ms; entries 380 ms ease-out with a 60 ms stagger; reduced motion keeps fades, drops movement, and stills the presence.

Faces: recommendation

  • Recommended: option B, the warm human face, but only while Q speaks on the Q page stage at 160 px or more. Everywhere else (the dock, the 52 px mark, Calm motion) Q is option A: no face, expression carried by motion.
  • Why the old face read as a demon: hollow dark sockets, bright staring eyes and edge-traced features. B avoids all three: an evenly sampled skin lit by a painted tone map, thin lines of light for lids, brows and lips, a closed smile, level brows, blinking, and a lower lip that moves with the voice.

Flags for the lead

  • Scores out of 10 conflict with ADR 0017’s ban on “fit meters” and match percentages. The mockups use a number with words and reasons, never a percentage or a meter; B1–B4 need an ADR amendment to allow it.
  • A face on Q conflicts with ADR 0017’s “face or character imagery” line. ADRs 0045 and 0049 already ship a face; option B needs that recorded.
  • Black and gold is more than an accent: today’s brand editor changes one colour. The preset also sets neutrals, the menu bar and Q’s light, so the brand-theme feature needs a preset layer.
