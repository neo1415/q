01 | EXECUTIVE SUMMARY



What InvestIQ Is
InvestIQ is FDN Capital Partners' investment-readiness system for determining how
investable a business is, identifying what could prevent it from raising capital, and
helping management fix those gaps before approaching investors.
InvestIQ is designed to go beyond pitch-deck preparation. Its purpose is to assess the underlying quality, readiness and
credibility of a business from an investor's perspective, then provide a structured pathway for improvement.



The central question
If an investor looked at this business today, what would make them invest, what would make them hesitate, and
what would make them walk away?



Why it matters
•   Too many businesses approach investors before the business itself is ready for institutional scrutiny.
•   Readiness gaps often sit beneath the pitch deck: governance, financial reporting, ownership, team, capital
    structure, valuation, diligence and commercial quality.
•   InvestIQ gives FDN a repeatable framework for diagnosing those issues before the market does.
•   Over time, the methodology can become proprietary intellectual property that supports advisory work, institutional
    partnerships and CapitalQ.

A business can have a strong idea, credible founders, real customers and an attractive market, yet still be uninvestable
at the point it enters the market for capital. Investors evaluate the entire enterprise, not only the story presented in a
deck.

Assess → Prepare → Position → Connect → Execute
    01      ASSESS Understand the business as an investor would.
            •    Business model and market
            •    Traction and commercial evidence
            •    Financial performance and forecasts
            •    Team and governance

            •    Diligence coordination
            •    Negotiation support

Valuation as a module
Valuation should sit inside InvestIQ as one component of the investment case, not as a standalone substitute for
readiness. FDN's valuation work should triangulate appropriate methods based on lifecycle stage and available
evidence, including comparable companies, precedent transactions, revenue or EBITDA multiples, DCF where visibility
supports it, peer-relative analysis, dilution math and negotiated fundraising pricing.

07 | COMMERCIAL MODEL & GUARDRAILS



How InvestIQ Can Create Value
InvestIQ can serve as an entry point into FDN's wider capital journey, with diagnostic work leading into readiness
improvement, capital strategy, transaction preparation and, where appropriate, fundraising execution.

Commercial architecture under consideration
•   Initial diagnostic / InvestIQ assessment
•   Readiness sprint or improvement engagement
•   Capital strategy and transaction preparation
•   Fundraising or capital execution mandate
•   Potential institutional licensing or cohort-based delivery over time

Critical guardrails
•   Paying for InvestIQ must never guarantee investor access, investment approval or fundraising success.
•   Assessment and investor selection should remain independent enough to protect credibility.
•   A business may be advised not to raise equity if debt, strategic capital, internal restructuring or delayed fundraising
    is more appropriate.
•   Scores should never create false precision. Evidence quality and investment judgment must remain visible.
•   Different company stages and capital instruments may require different readiness models.

09 | INTERNAL POSITIONING



The Strategic Opportunity
Investor access is valuable, but it is not highly defensible on its own. FDN's long-term advantage can become its ability
to determine whether a business is genuinely ready for capital, improve that business against institutional standards,
and generate better-quality opportunities for investors.

InvestIQ can evolve from a consulting methodology into a standardised assessment,
then into software-enabled infrastructure used by businesses, accelerators, banks,
DFIs, family offices and investment platforms.

Working one-sentence definition
InvestIQ is FDN Capital Partners' investment-readiness system for assessing how investable a business is,
identifying what could prevent it from raising capital, and helping management fix those gaps before
approaching investors.

Commercial expression
Before you raise capital, know what investors will see.
InvestIQ assesses the business, identifies the gaps standing between it and investment, and helps management
become ready for the right capital.
