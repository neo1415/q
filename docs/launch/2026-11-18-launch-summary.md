# Going live: costs and decisions

## What it costs to run

|                     | Lean launch                                  | Normal                                        | Comfortable                                      |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Who it's for        | Today's traffic, a few hundred users a month | About 1,000 people a day (100 online at once) | About 10,000 people a day (1,000 online at once) |
| Monthly cost        | **About $460**                               | **About $9,500**                              | **About $70,000 to $90,000**                     |
| Subscription income | About $0 to start                            | About $36,000                                 | About $360,000                                   |

Most of the bigger amounts is AI usage. It only goes up when more people use the product, and the subscription prices are set so each paying user covers their own usage with a profit. The fixed running cost is small. Even at the "Comfortable" level, servers, database and email together are under $3,000 a month.

There are daily spending limits built in, so a bad day can't empty the account. We raise them as income grows.

## First month: $1,000

This covers the first month at the lean level, plus a reserve of about $250. Lawyer's fees are not included.
