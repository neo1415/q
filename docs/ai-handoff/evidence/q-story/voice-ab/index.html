How I ranked them

I can’t listen, so this ranking comes from measurements, not taste. Your ear decides.

  1. Brian has the widest pitch movement of all six, so it’s the least monotone. It performs the sigh, and every word is clear.
  2. Lily is the most expressive female voice, if Q should stay female.
  3. George is close behind and is labelled a storyteller voice.
  4. Sarah is the flattest ElevenLabs voice here, which fits the feedback that she sounds like a basic voice with emotions added.
  5. OpenAI marin and cedar are the most monotone. They ran about 25% faster than the pace they were asked for, performed no sigh, and in marin’s take the transcript is missing “Do I trust this business?”. They also can’t follow per-line delivery cues, and they return no word timings, so the story’s cues would need an extra alignment step.
“Pitch range” is the spread of the voice’s pitch in semitones (5th to 95th percentile): higher means more movement, lower means flatter. “Words heard right” is a speech-to-text transcript matched against the script.
