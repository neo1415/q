## Live checks after deployment (ready to run)

Read-only, through `scripts/handoff/live/hosted-read.mjs` (set `NODE_USE_ENV_PROXY=1`). Replace `<deployed_at>` with the deployment time (UTC).

1. Answer-model calls per run, before against after (rows 16 and 5):

   ```sql
   select (occurred_at >= '<deployed_at>') as after_deploy,
          count(distinct q_run_id) as runs,
          round(count(*)::numeric / nullif(count(distinct q_run_id), 0), 2) as answer_calls_per_run,
          percentile_cont(0.5) within group (order by latency_ms) as p50_ms
   from ai_ops.model_usage
   where task_class in ('NORMAL_DIALOGUE', 'EVIDENCE_SYNTHESIS', 'COMPARISON')
     and q_run_id is not null and success
     and occurred_at >= timestamp '<deployed_at>' - interval '3 days'
   group by 1 order by 1;
   ```

2. q-api logs: `q answered an arrival follow-up from the arrival snapshot` with `"early":true` and its `totalMs` (row 22); the answer phases log (`round0`, `round1`) on a "what are the risks with <company>" turn should show one tool round where it showed two.
