- Comparisons are tables; summaries are a summary artifact.
- Research is arranged as it arrives.

### Design pass (which skill drove which change)

- **frontend-design:** summary points are not a sequence, so the numbers were removed; no all-caps labels; one entrance moment instead of scattered effects.
- **emil-design-eng / animate:** preview frames enter with a 200 ms fade and a 6 px rise on `--cq-ease` (CSS `@starting-style` transition, interruptible). Reduced motion keeps the fade only. The summary highlight transitions colour properties only. No `scale(0)`, no `transition: all`.
- **accessibility:** the spoken summary point is bold as well as lit, and marked `aria-current` (never colour alone). Image credits use secondary text for contrast. Avatars are decorative beside a visible name. The preview region is `aria-busy` and named.
- **apple-design:** spatial consistency (the real cards land in the preview frames' places). No springs, because there are no gestures. The working line is treated as status feedback.

#### Round 3 (2026-10-21, `speed/artifacts-r3`)

`review-animations` is not installed; `improve-animations` was used for the audit instead.

- **frontend-design:** the working line no longer joins state and stage with a middle dot; the stage sits on its own line under the state, matching the arrival's shape. TABLE figures use tabular numerals; header cells keep a minimum width so names do not wrap letter by letter.
- **emil-design-eng / animate / improve-animations:** the audit found two motion defects. The lit summary point changed weight, which reflowed its words; it now keeps its weight and the rail thickens by `transform: scaleX` (200 ms, `--cq-ease`, colour-only under reduced motion). A new stage swapped words instantly; it now fades in by opacity (140 ms `--cq-motion-fast`, `@starting-style`, kept under reduced motion because a fade is not movement). No other motion was added.
- **accessibility:** the TABLE frame becomes a named, focusable region only when it scrolls sideways (WCAG 2.1.1), and is no tab stop otherwise. TABLE header links and PICTURE credit links are 44 px targets with visible focus. Claim/inference labels and the "Not on file yet" list moved from tertiary to secondary text for contrast on the lit background. The lit point is told by shape and `aria-current`, not colour alone.
- **apple-design:** spatial consistency between the thread's working line and the arrival's. No springs: none of these respond to a gesture.
