## Source contract and decisions requiring approval

Read with the original PADL Chapter 4 #62–#76, decision #142 on qualitative uncertainty, and Product Specification 4.3–4.11; source reconciliation and exact extract links are in [INVESTIQ-RECONCILIATION.md](INVESTIQ-RECONCILIATION.md). Specification 4.3 requires independent evidence/reasoning/confidence for the Core Seven and completion of each applicable dimension before a final assessment. Sections 4.5/4.7 distinguish unsupported, missing, contradictory and inapplicable evidence; 4.10 preserves immutable history. Section 4.11 requires one underlying institutional truth with authorized role-specific delivery.

Recommendation: assess all seven dimensions, report Business Quality separately from fundraising readiness, and retain a qualitative Overall Institutional Assessment. An experimental seven-dimension aggregate is an internal sensitivity diagnostic, never the user-facing headline. Experimental Business Quality uses the first five dimensions below; governance and capital strategy remain independently visible and can produce material risk flags without silently changing business strength. This interpretation of PADL #62 versus #63 / Specification 4.4 needs explicit leadership approval before production. An alternative approved composition must receive its own immutable methodology version; do not relabel old snapshots.

Readiness, company–mandate fit, candidate rank, actual interest, relationship stage, probability and commercial outcomes remain separate existing systems. An out-of-mandate company can have strong Business Quality. No interest or investment probability is inferred from any proposed number.

## Observation contract and anchored rubrics

Each observation requires a canonical company, criterion ID, authorized evidence reference, original source family, reporting period, units, observation timestamp, applicable stage/sector/model, source class and verification state. AI may propose an extraction and rubric anchor with a quoted source span and rationale. Deterministic code checks the contract; contradictory/unclear observations require resolution rather than model-selected final arithmetic. An unsupported extraction, invented evidence ID or inaccessible source is refused.

Criterion states: `OBSERVED`, `UNKNOWN`, `AWAITING_VERIFICATION`, `CONFLICTED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`. Absence is not an observed zero. NOT_APPLICABLE requires a versioned context rule and recorded reason, never a founder's checkbox alone. A negative observed anchor needs affirmative evidence of weakness, not merely a missing document. Stage definitions and specific metric thresholds belong to a separately calibrated versioned rubric catalog.

Experimental anchors are integers 0–4: 0 material demonstrated weakness; 1 partial progress with demonstrated weakness; 2 credible stage-appropriate baseline; 3 stronger than baseline with corroboration; 4 sustained or exceptionally strong stage-appropriate performance supported by evidence. Scores 1/3 require reasons showing which adjacent anchor requirements are and are not met. Do not create decimals by model intuition. The following criterion-specific 0/2/4 anchors are the initial review rubric, not an assertion that generic thresholds work for every sector.

| Dimension / criterion                                          | 0: affirmative weakness                                            | 2: stage-appropriate baseline                                                            | 4: strong, evidenced performance                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Founder & Leadership / relevant capability                     | Demonstrated inability to perform essential role                   | Relevant capability demonstrated in current work                                         | Repeated relevant delivery and independently corroborated capability              |

| Market Opportunity / competition and timing                    | Critical substitute/regulatory constraint invalidates thesis       | Identified alternatives and credible differentiated timing                               | Demonstrated customer preference and defensible timing advantage                  |
| Product & Technology / validation                              | Product demonstrably fails essential use case                      | Stage-appropriate prototype/pilot achieves essential use case                            | Repeated successful use with independent validation                               |
| Product & Technology / defensibility                           | Claimed critical right/capability shown absent or unusable         | Credible advantage with source-linked ownership/technical basis                          | Demonstrated durable advantage appropriate to sector, not patent-count proxy      |

Pre-seed revenue and mature retention metrics may be unavailable or not yet applicable; credible pilot/problem validation remains assessable. Growth-stage absence of retention evidence is UNKNOWN, while measured poor retention is negative OBSERVED. Regulated-sector essential permissions remain applicable at early stages when law requires them. SaaS retention, marketplace liquidity, deep-tech validation and service repeatability need distinct metric definitions rather than reused revenue rubrics. Operational evidence supports relevant criteria or deeper diligence; it adds no eighth dimension. Growth observations map to market/product/commercial/financial criteria; shared evidence must not multiply a single signal across all four.

## Evidence, conflicts and freshness

Proposed source reliability ceilings: independently verified primary records 0.95; attributable primary records not independently verified 0.75; attributable independent third-party evidence 0.70; public company statement 0.45; founder claim 0.35; unsupported promotional assertion 0.15; AI inference without underlying evidence 0.00. These are calibration parameters, not probabilities. An uploaded spreadsheet is not verified just because it was uploaded. Public accessibility is not proof of accuracy. Authenticated access is an authorization fact, not evidence verification.

Evidence authority is criterion-specific: a bank statement is strong for cash, weak for product desirability. A material qualifier absent from the excerpt makes the observation pending, not strong. Deduplicate by original source/claim family, not filename or URL; ten copies of a deck have no more reliability than one. Independent corroboration can add a bounded 0.15 times the second independent source reliability, capped at 1.00. Generated summaries do not become independent corroboration.

Freshness multiplies confidence only: `2 ** (-ageDays / halfLifeDays)`, with future timestamps rejected. Suggested half-lives: liquidity 90 days; commercial 180; product 365; leadership/market 365; ownership/legal 730 unless a material event supersedes it. These require calibration. Historical financial periods retain their historical meaning; freshness does not turn old revenue into today's revenue or reduce demonstrated business strength simply because time passed.

Resolve different units, periods, cohort definitions, cash/accrual bases and superseded versions before declaring contradiction. Genuine unresolved material contradiction marks that criterion CONFLICTED, preserves both references and reporting periods, and withholds its numeric point. Do not select the more flattering source or assume fraud. Where authoritative evidence resolves the conflict, preserve the resolution rationale and old snapshot. Confidence and uncertainty change; Business Quality changes only when the resolved business observation changes.

## Deterministic experimental arithmetic

Three criteria per dimension initially have equal within-dimension influence. Context-specific applicability changes that denominator only through documented rules. Each observed anchor maps to `anchor * 2.5` on a 0–10 experimental scale. Dimension strength is the weighted mean of known observations, explicitly labelled provisional where coverage is incomplete. Empty known sets yield null, never a default 6, 8.8, 0 or neutral midpoint.

The dimension's lower/upper uncertainty bounds allocate every unresolved applicable criterion its possible 0/10 value under its original weight. A conditional observed mean can therefore stay high while its uncertainty remains wide. This is an evidence interval, not a statistical confidence interval. Showing only the known mean would reward selective withholding; keep interval, coverage, evidence flags and finalization status inseparable.

Proposed context profiles, in Core Seven order, before normalization:

| Stage    | Leadership | Market | Product | Commercial | Financial | Governance | Capital |
| -------- | ---------- | ------ | ------- | ---------- | --------- | ---------- | ------- |
| Pre-seed | 24         | 22     | 22      | 10         | 10        | 6          | 6       |
| Seed     | 18         | 18     | 20      | 18         | 14        | 7          | 5       |
| Growth   | 12         | 12     | 14      | 24         | 22        | 10         | 6       |

Sector/model adjustments are explicit bounded multiplicative profiles, not an LLM-generated percentage: proposed regulated-sector governance ×1.5; capital-intensive product and financial ×1.25; recurring-revenue commercial ×1.15. Normalize after composition; cap any unapproved multiplier outside 0.5–2.0 and reject unsupported contexts rather than defaulting to seed. Context changes require evidence and a new snapshot. Geography changes legal applicability, not the intrinsic quality of a founder. Additional stage/sector/model combinations are unsupported until calibrated.

Business Quality uses first-five normalized weights as the proposed interpretation above. The internal seven-dimension diagnostic uses all seven. Both retain unresolved criteria in interval/coverage denominators rather than silently increasing the impact of selectively available evidence. Evidence reliability determines whether an observation can support finalization and the confidence of its influence; it does not directly turn uncertainty into negative merit.

Dimension confidence = weighted coverage × mean deduplicated, authority-adjusted, freshness-adjusted reliability of known criteria. Aggregate confidence = context-weighted mean of dimension confidence. Unresolved/conflicted criteria have zero confidence contribution and remain visible. Never multiply strength by confidence to create a third ambiguous quality score.

Proposed numeric finalization gates: complete applicable Business Quality criterion coverage (1.00); applicable criterion coverage ≥0.80 overall; every applicable dimension coverage ≥0.50; aggregate confidence ≥0.65; no unresolved material contradiction; all material source references valid and authorized. These are safety hypotheses for expert review, not approved product thresholds. Missing gates mean `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` with provisional dimension observations and precise next evidence requests; no final headline/score. Unknown coverage itself must remain distinct from low confidence in known facts.

Compute at full precision; round internal experiment reports only once, half-up to one decimal for experimental strength and integer percent for the experimental confidence index. PADL #142 generally requires qualitative user-facing confidence (high/moderate/low) rather than unsupported numeric precision; these uncalibrated experiment percentages must not become user-facing certainty claims. Qualitative band thresholds require separate calibration and approval. Stable tie handling uses the underlying values and explicit evidence differences; never random jitter to make companies look differentiated. Do not change production fit rounding. Persisted experiment snapshots contain methodology/profile/rubric versions, as-of time, original observations, context, assumptions, conflicts, input hash and arithmetic trace. No `Date.now()` in deterministic evaluation; the caller supplies the snapshot time.

## Sensitivity, validation and approval contract

For each fixture, perturb each dimension influence ±20%, renormalize, and report the resulting diagnostic range without changing the stored baseline. Also test alternate legitimate context profiles, removal of the strongest evidence source, stale evidence, contradiction resolution, one changed rubric anchor and duplicate-source injection. If a small defensible perturbation reverses the apparent order, report instability rather than claiming meaningful 0.1-point differences.

Use a blinded expert-scored, permissioned calibration set spanning stage, sector/model, geography and evidence completeness. Two independent reviewers provide anchored judgments and adjudicated disagreements. Measure inter-reviewer agreement, criterion error, material false reassurance, interval coverage, confidence calibration against factual assessment reliability, cohort disparity and ranking stability. Validate on held-out cases; keep input provenance and prevent outcome leakage. Investment success/failure is not the target label for this institutional assessment and does not authorize investment probability claims.

Mandatory scenario contract:

| Scenario                                         | Required result                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pre-seed, strong product, no revenue             | No zero solely for absent revenue; stage-appropriate validation and explicit gaps                  |
| Seed, real traction, weak governance             | Strong commercial observation and separate negative governance; no hidden compensating blend       |
| Growth, revenue, bad unit economics              | Revenue does not erase negative financial observation                                              |
| Impressive unsupported claims                    | Low confidence/provisional or insufficient evidence; no final high-quality endorsement             |
| Contradictory financials                         | Preserve both references; no invented resolution; withhold conflicted point and finalization       |
| Insufficient evidence                            | Null final score, wide bounds, specific missing information; no fallback number                    |
| Outside the investor mandate                     | Objective quality unchanged; existing fit remains separately poor                                  |
| Strong Business Quality, poor fit                | Distinct outputs, no investor-specific Business Quality                                            |
| Weak Business Quality, strong fit                | Strong mandate fit does not manufacture business merit                                             |
| Similar companies, materially different evidence | Similar observed strengths can coexist with different confidence/intervals; no forced merit jitter |

Adversarial tests must prove duplicate uploads, marketing volume, reordered sources, selectively withheld weak evidence, unsupported context upgrades, copied verified labels, expired authorizations and adversarial instructions inside evidence cannot improve a final assessment. Permission tests ensure private founder evidence never silently enters an investor ranking or explanation. One assessment truth does not confer universal access to its private source material.

Leadership review must resolve Business Quality composition, rubrics/context coverage, weights, confidence parameters, finalization gates, calibration standards, publication language and methodology owner. Only then can a separate production implementation reuse existing canonical evidence/context/assessment contracts with immutable snapshots, RLS, permission tests and normal release gates. This document creates no second business service or production scoring source of truth.

## Offline prototype and observed design correction

Run `node scripts/handoff/live/investiq-proposed-evaluation.mjs`. It accepts only its synthetic offline fixtures, makes no network/database/provider calls and imports no application business services. All ten required scenarios and ten invariants pass; each result includes experimental coverage/confidence/bounds and ±20% influence sensitivity. These scenarios verify arithmetic and separation, not real-company calibration or historical 8.8 provenance.

The evidence-withholding regression first failed: an observed weakness removed from the known set raised the provisional mean to 7.5 while the original 80% overall coverage gate still allowed a numeric final score. The proposal now requires complete applicable Business Quality criterion coverage for numeric finalization. The same regression passes with a null final score and retained uncertainty bounds. A future authorized snapshot adapter must also preserve previously observed adverse evidence and explicit supersession history; that persistence behavior is not implemented by this isolated experiment.

Prototype limits: supplied integer anchors stand in for extraction/adjudication; source authority is synthetic; only pre-seed retention before live use has an implemented inapplicability rule; whole-dimension exclusions and additional contexts are rejected. It does not test production permissions, source retrieval, exposure controls, role delivery, calibration or evidence ingestion. Production adoption still requires the decisions and integration above.

**Current implementation status:** proposed design and synthetic arithmetic prototype locally exercised; numerical constants uncalibrated; production integration absent. The real 6.0/3.5 fit audit and unresolved historical 8.8 remain in SCORE-LINEAGE.md.
