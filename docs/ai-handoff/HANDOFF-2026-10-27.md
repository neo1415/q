# Handoff — 2026-10-27 (voice onboarding rebuilt as a conversation)

Read `HANDOFF-2026-10-26.md` for the state before this. This file covers one
evening's work, prompted by the owner's live test of voice onboarding with a
new investor (Tiara, The Vaultlyne) at 20:20 UTC.

## What the owner saw, and the causes (from q-api logs and the database)

1. **"How do you invest?" as the first words, with no introduction.** The
   opening was an 11 s model turn that read the investor-type step's form
   label. There was 16 s of silence before it.
2. **Answers were lost, and Q said "I can't save that".** Naming the firm
   created the organisation mid-call and moved the person to its tenant. The
   live voice line was bound to the pre-organisation (personal bootstrap)
   tenant, so every delegation, transcript and usage call after that returned 404. From then on the voice model talked alone.
3. **"Series A" refused.** Speech-to-text ran a question into the answer that
   followed ("What do you mean by that question okay I mainly do series A…").
   A regex treated any sentence opening with "what" as a question.
4. **Live calls kept almost none of the person's words.** This was found
   while building the fix. The web relay's transcript schema refused the
   `timings` field the Q API accepts, and the browser attaches timings to the
   batch after someone speaks. Those batches were dropped silently: 1 line of
   the person's against 51 of Q's, 14 against 234.
5. **"Onboarding 4/4"** had been scored on typed onboarding with existing
   accounts. Voice and brand-new accounts were never covered (DECISIONS 41).

## What changed (branch `claude/nice-goldberg-d6vax8`, merged to `speed/q-oct20`)

- `f7e710b2b`: a live line follows the same person from the personal
  bootstrap tenant into their first organisation. Any other tenant change is
  still refused (tests both ways).
- `338fb55c4`: the opening-word regex is gone from all three places it
  decided. Whether words ask is the turn reader's `lookup`, read by meaning.
- `56de4276b`, `348fb2f3f`, `d674b5af1`: **conversation-first voice
  onboarding** (DECISIONS 39), in `apps/q-api/src/voice/onboarding-conversation.ts`:
  - The opening is Q's real introduction plus a plain first question, built
    by code. For a brand-new conversation the typed opening uses it too.
    Research on the firm or company starts with it.
  - On the live line, Q holds the conversation from a checklist (`briefOf`):
    needed vs optional, what is saved, what was found. Answers are no longer
    delegated one by one.
  - The transcript is stitched into whole utterances in the onboarding thread
    (`onboarding.interview_turns`, channel VOICE).
  - New prompt `ONBOARDING_TRANSCRIPT_READER` v1 (STRUCTURED_EXTRACTION) reads
    the whole thread about 2.5 s after each thing the person says, and again
    when the line ends. What it finds is saved through the port's own
    `record` / `setAside`. Their quote must be in their own lines; an
    unchanged value is not rewritten; a changed one is a new version with
    history kept. If the reading fails, nothing is saved and the thread is
    kept.
  - When the voice delegates (what is saved so far, stop for now, finish),
    everything heard is saved first, then the record is read back from the
    database. Finishing goes through `finish`, which confirms the journey's
    review steps on their word.
  - 36 plain spoken questions were added (`packages/onboarding/src/application/spoken-questions.ts`).
  - Rollback: `CQ_ONBOARDING_VOICE_CONVERSATION=off` on q-api returns the
    guided interview.
- `a40a2224a` (web, merged): "Continue later" on the voice stage. `end()`
  flushes the transcript first (capped at 1.5 s). Upload findings go into the
  live call as a quiet note, with no tap. A document reading card shows a
  skeleton.
- `21d8fe617`: the relay accepts `timings`, and the browser bounds them.

Tests: q-api 2,592 passed, web 3,505 passed, packages 2,459 passed. Root,
q-api and web typechecks have 0 errors; eslint and prettier are clean on the
changed files.
