## 6. Develop InvestIQ Quantitative Methodology v1

The approved sources define the qualitative architecture but do not contain a complete approved mathematical scoring algorithm.

Treat that as a methodology-design task, not permission to pretend one already exists.

Develop a serious, institutionally defensible proposed methodology that includes:

- Core Seven dimension-specific assessment rubrics.
- Subcriteria and contextual applicability.
- Stage, sector and business-model adjustment rules.
- Dynamic contextual weighting.
- Evidence quality and reliability treatment.
- Handling of missing, contradictory and outdated evidence.
- Intelligence Confidence calculation or calibration approach.
- Aggregation and rounding rules.
- Score confidence and interpretability.
- Historical methodology versioning.
- Benchmarking strategy.
- Manipulation and gaming resistance.
- Calibration and consistency tests.

Research relevant institutional investment evaluation methods and credible quantitative approaches when useful. Distinguish external reference methods from Capital Q's own product decisions.

Avoid arbitrary fixed percentages presented as institutional truth.

The methodology should be deterministic and reproducible where numerical scores are calculated, while using AI for evidence extraction, contextual assessment and well-grounded reasoning.

Produce an implementation-ready candidate, with precise assumptions, test vectors and sensitivity analyses.

**Important authority boundary:** You may independently design, prototype, test and recommend a methodology. Do not label new quantitative business rules as officially approved FDN intellectual property, or silently replace existing production scoring semantics, until the methodology has appropriate product or investment leadership approval.

Continue shipping independently verifiable correctness fixes that do not require those decisions.

## 7. Separate InvestIQ from investor matching

Design and enforce a clear separation between:

**InvestIQ:** An institutional assessment of the business.

**Investment Readiness:** Its preparedness for the relevant capital objective.

**Intelligence Confidence:** The reliability and completeness of the assessment.

**Investor Thesis Match:** Fit against a specific investor's declared investment criteria.

**Discovery Ranking:** The ordering of opportunities for a particular authorized context.

**Investor Interest:** Actual investor behavior, not a predicted score.

**Relationship State:** The actual progression of the investment relationship.

Ensure internal types, APIs, tool outputs and user interfaces maintain these distinctions.

Never collapse them into one generic company score.

## 8. Protect institutional truth and permissions

Preserve the distinction between founder-private intelligence, investor-private information and authorized shared company evidence.

Confidential founder disclosures must not silently influence investor-facing rankings or be exposed through explanations, derived results or generated reports without appropriate authority.

Maintain one consistent versioned institutional assessment while enforcing lawful and legitimate evidence visibility.

An assessment shown to an investor must be supported by evidence that can legitimately influence that investor-facing conclusion.

Enforce this below the presentation layer.
