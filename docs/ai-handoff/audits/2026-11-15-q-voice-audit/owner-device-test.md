# Real-device voice test for the owner (production `651a3bd50`)

Audio cannot be heard from the build machine (no UDP), so these checks need your phone and laptop. Each step says what should happen; note anything different with the time, and I will match it to the new voice telemetry (run id, words handed to the voice, audio start and end) recorded for every line since `651a3bd50`.

Use your investor account on a laptop (Chrome) first, then your founder account on a phone (Safari or Chrome). Allow the microphone.

## A. The screen while Q speaks (voice room)

1. Start a voice session on Q home. Before anything is shown, Q's presence is large and centred.
2. Ask: "Show me my strongest fits." When the cards appear, Q's presence moves to the side (laptop) or a small dock at the bottom (phone). The cards are the main thing on screen; captions are two discreet lines and never cover a card or a button.
3. While Q is still speaking, scroll down through the cards. **The page must stay where you put it.** If a new result arrives out of view, a "New result" button appears instead of the page moving.
4. Watch Q's light while it speaks: a gentle, smooth swell, not a jump on every syllable. With "reduce motion" on in your device settings, it stays still.

## B. The voice says only what Q decided

5. Ask a question that takes a while ("How solid is Ajopot's revenue?"). You hear at most one "Working on it." — never a stack of them, never small talk, never "I'm checking what's public" (public search is out of credit, so that line should not play), never "coming up".
6. Compare what you hear with the written answer on screen: same facts, same figures. A long analysis is spoken as a short summary; the detail is on screen.
7. Say "say that again". Q repeats its last answer word for word, quickly, without starting a new search.
8. Ask about a figure in euros (e.g. your cheque range). Amounts are spoken correctly ("fifty thousand euros", not "fifty billion").

## C. Natural pauses and follow-ups

9. Speak with a pause and a filler mid-sentence: "Which of these… um… actually have revenue?" Q treats it as one question (one answer, not two).
10. Right after an answer, ask short follow-ups: "Why the first one?", "And the second?", "Go ahead." These should come back noticeably faster than the first answer and refer to the right companies.
11. Say "still waiting?" while Q is working. Q keeps working on your question; it does not drop it.

## D. Conditions and continuity

12. Investor: "Strongest fits leaving out companies I'm already talking to" — none of your relationships appear, and Q says it left them out.
13. Founder: "Which investors fit us, leaving out the ones I'm already talking to?" then "And the poor fits?" — no investor you talk to (e.g. Zino) in the first list; the second list is different, worst first.
14. Ask about a company in your pipeline ("Is Nixo connected?"). Q states the stage exactly as your pipeline shows.

## E. Approvals by voice

15. Ask Q to draft a short message to a **fictional** contact. Say "yes". Q reads the message back first; only a second "yes" sends it. (This double step is the open decision: tell me if one "yes" should be enough when the card is on screen.)
