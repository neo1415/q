## Ranked waste and fixes

**1. Voice trailing silence: about $4/month (12% of voice). Owner decision; not changed here.** Lines stay open until 3 minutes of silence pass (`apps/q-api/src/voice/live/config.ts` `idleMs`). Over 7 days, 20 of 173 billed minutes came after the last spoken turn. Proposed fix: close after 60–90 s of silence, after a spoken "still there?". This changes behaviour, so it needs the owner's word.

**2. Dialogue prompt-cache misses: about $5–15/month (scales with use). Needs one approved live check.** v37 calls since 18 Nov 13:17: 30 first-answer calls averaging 36.9k input and 11.5k cached; 15 of them read nothing from the cache. Uncached input is $0.0046 a call, against about $0.0012 at a 75% hit rate.

The installed SDK (openai 7.21.0) shows that `gpt-5.6` caches only at breakpoints:

- one implicit breakpoint per request;
- up to 3 explicit breakpoints via `prompt_cache_breakpoint`;
- a 30-minute minimum TTL.

Proposed fix: put an explicit breakpoint on the developer message that ends the stable prefix (shared instructions, then tools, then the person's charter tail; `providers/openai.ts` `toInput`). Each later turn from the same person then reuses that prefix. Not implemented: whether it works, and whether a breakpoint write costs extra, can only be shown with a paid call. Next step: one small approved call pair, reading `cached_input_tokens`.

**3. knowledge.reconcile never converges: DB load, $0 direct. Needs a migration (stop point).**

- 426 of 585 companies stay "due" forever: listed, but `source_version <> core.companies.version`.
- Cause: `knowledge.refresh_company_profile` (`20261220181000_knowledge_projections.sql`) updates only `where p.fingerprint is distinct from excluded.fingerprint`. When the company's version moves with no fingerprint change, `source_version` never catches up, so every 10-minute run rebuilds all 426 profiles and writes nothing.
- Cost: about 1.0 s per run (5,730 runs, 5,760 s since 16 Sep; third-largest statement by total time) and about 61k useless profile builds a day.
- Proposed migration: in that function, when the fingerprint is unchanged, still set `source_version = c.version`, without taking a new `version`, so downstream readers see no change.
- Implemented now: the reconciler backs off once it is idle (below). With the migration, its steady cost falls to about one run an hour.

**4. Reader same-model hedge: at most about $2/month. Kept for latency.**

- 92 cancelled hedge calls in 3 days, about 19% of turns. They fire at 2.5 s and are cancelled after a median of about 0.5 s.
- The ledger records them at $0, but the provider may bill prefill on a cancelled request: up to about 13k tokens, roughly $0.0026 each.
- The hedge protects turn latency (slow first attempts), so it stays.

**5. Reader prompt order: under $1/month. Not worth an eval cycle.** The reader does not use `sharedInstructions`, but its cached share was already 72% on 19 Nov (9.6k of 13.3k). A v95 with shared instructions, like v37, would save cents.

**6. Connection churn: DB load, config not code.**

- `DISCARD ALL`: 67,922 calls averaging 88.6 ms, 6,018 s in all (largest by total time).
- `pgbouncer.get_auth`: 379k calls.
- `pg_type` lookups: 66k calls.

These come from pooled connections being recycled. The pool lifetime and idle settings are the lever; the 19 Nov workers-pool slice already watches this.

**7. Pollers: fine.** Over a 198 s window, `pgmq.read` ran 7.9 times a minute and the outbox poll about 2 times a minute; both are already on 30 s idle backoff. Total measured background execution was 1.03 s across 464 calls, about 0.5% of one core.

The chunk-embedding sweep's withheld count costs 520 ms every 10 minutes. It is minor; left as is.

## Implemented in this pass

**Knowledge reconciler backoff** (`apps/q-api/src/composition/idle-sweep.ts`, wired in `main.ts`).

- Every 10 minutes as before. After 3 consecutive runs that touch nothing, the wait grows to 20, then 40, then at most 60 minutes. The first run that finds work, or any failed run, drops it back to 10 minutes.
- **Bound:** a change the triggers missed is repaired within 60 minutes (it was 10). Normal changes are unaffected: same-transaction triggers keep projections current.
- Tests: `apps/q-api/test/idle-sweep.test.ts`.

**Stuck runs are unchanged.** The orphaned-run sweep still runs every 30 s with a 60 s engine heartbeat window, so a run whose engine died is failed within about 90 s. In-flight silence (15 min) and paused (24 h) windows are unchanged.
