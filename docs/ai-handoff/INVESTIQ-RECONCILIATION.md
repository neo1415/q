## What is contradictory, unresolved or merely missing

1. **Source identity defect:** duplicate decision numbers are a documentation ambiguity. The stable IDs above resolve citation identity without changing business semantics.
2. **Historical taxonomy evolution:** Locked #016 lists ten dimensions; later Chapter 4 #63 and Product Specification 4.3 prescribe seven. The founder's new directive explicitly preserves Core Seven and requires legacy mapping. Keep original documents and historical outputs; implement an additive corrigendum and approved mappings rather than rewriting history.
3. **Unresolved business aggregation choice:** Chapter 4 #62 says governance, readiness and capital strategy are complementary rather than artificially changing Business Quality, while #63 describes all seven as a comprehensive view of quality and 4.4 weights all seven into the Overall Institutional Assessment. Recommendation: distinguish the qualitative headline from Business Quality, assess all seven independently, and propose precisely which subcriteria contribute to each supporting output. Leadership must approve the production numerical contract. Do not silently resolve this by averaging seven scores or by eliminating governance.
4. **Implementation gap:** current readiness uses eight legacy private pillars; no governed Core Seven FDN numerical evaluator was found. This is not proof that the PADL is internally contradictory. Existing fit and readiness services remain useful in their actual roles.
5. **Validation gap:** current 6.0/3.5 ties are reproduced against 42 authorized canonical fit profiles; historical 8.8 provenance is still unresolved. A mathematical fixture that can produce 8.8 does not establish its historical producer.
6. **Not a conflict:** the Final System Review requires `Readiness ≠ Quality ≠ Fit ≠ Interest ≠ Probability ≠ Outcome` and prohibits private founder information from silently influencing investor ranking. GateQ's supplementary document separately lists exact Q Score methodology, weighting and governance among “Features to Design Before Building.” None supplies approved FDN numeric rubrics.
