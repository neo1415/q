# Owner directive (16 Nov 2026): complete demo evidence and data-room seeding

The owner's words, condensed. Binding, below the PADL and locked architecture.

**Objective.** Too many demo companies have only founder statements and a deck, so Q keeps saying "unverified" and cannot show its depth. Audit the demo data, build the missing evidence, put it into the real product (storage, extraction, indexing, evidence model, data room, sharing) and prove Q uses it. Do not weaken the evidence hierarchy or fake genuine verification. No excess compliance screens or warnings.

1. **Inspect.**
   - Read the PADL, spec, FSR, V1, database, knowledge/RAG and security architecture, and the demo seed strategy.
   - Inventory every demo company, showcase first: Halia, TensorGate, Nixo.
   - For each, record documents, missing evidence, data-room state, extraction and indexing, claim/evidence links, visibility, and what Q can retrieve and cite.
   - List the investment questions Q cannot answer.
   - Distinguish missing evidence from broken retrieval and from genuine permission limits.
   - Don't regenerate evidence that already works.
2. **Design.**
   - Showcase companies get about 8–12 documents each; other companies get smaller packages.
   - Keep each company's identity and storyline.
   - Possible document types: corporate and ownership, financial and banking, commercial, fundraising, and operations or market.
3. **Generate synthetic documents.**
   - Mark every document "SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT - NOT A REAL FINANCIAL OR OFFICIAL RECORD", with a watermark and footer.
   - Use fictitious banks only. No imitation of real certificates, signatures or seals, and no real customer data.
   - Don't repeat "fictional" warnings in profiles, cards or Q answers.
   - Generate every related document from one deterministic source-of-truth fixture, so figures reconcile: revenue to invoices, cash/burn/runway, dated customer counts, ownership summing to 100%, projections separate from actuals.
   - Include a few deliberate contradictions, such as an old deck figure against new accounts, for Q to explain.
4. **Investors, GateQ and relationships.**
   - Enrich each investor with its mandate, constraints, cheque size, strategy, portfolio, decision process, diligence needs, notes, meeting history, relationship events and GateQ configuration.
   - Keep declared, observed and inferred apart.
   - Seed these GateQ scenarios: a strong application; unresolved questions; a genuine discrepancy; a founder correction with history kept; a submitted application with a frozen dossier; an answered request; a pending request.
5. **Populate the real systems.**
   - Upload through the supported ingestion and storage path.
   - Verify for every document: correct entity, storage location, tenant and owner; category and default visibility; owner retrieval; indexing and search; extraction; evidence links; citation and opening; documents and data-room UI; and sharing through the real workflow.
   - Founder documents stay private until shared. Seed some legitimate grants. Never make private documents public to satisfy a question.
6. **Q uses the evidence.** Q must be able to:
   - explain the business model; summarise revenue and performance; work out burn and runway;
   - show trends, compare periods, reconcile documents and explain discrepancies;
   - separate projections from actuals; assess customer concentration, ownership and the raise;
   - raise diligence questions, compare companies and write memos, with citations;
   - recommend next steps, prepare requests and follow up on unanswered requests.
     Synthetic evidence is never genuine verification, and a simulated verification state is never a real outcome. Mention limits only where they affect the decision. Missing verification never blocks exploratory analysis, but required locked gates stay.
7. **Live scenarios** on fictional accounts:
   - A: financial evidence (Halia).
   - B: deck against financials.
   - C: uncertainty reduction.
   - D: a private document shared through the workflow.
   - E: comparing Halia, TensorGate and Nixo, with mandate fit kept separate from business quality.
   - F: a GateQ application.
   - G: the founder's prioritised gaps.
   - H: voice, where the environment allows; never claim audio that was not tested.
8. **Seeding.** Deterministic and idempotent, with stable ids and a demo reset in line with the established strategy. Never touch non-demo tenants or real customer data. No unneeded model calls or new paid services.
9. **Output.**
   - Inventory and gaps; seed matrix; document inventory.
   - Consistency, ingestion and retrieval results; data-room and sharing checks; Q answers with citations.
   - Live acceptance; negative permission and cross-tenant tests.
   - Files, migrations, commands, tests and the deployed SHA; blockers and decisions.

Completion: an investor investigates Halia, TensorGate, Nixo and others, sees evidence-backed analysis, inspects documents, compares, requests diligence and progresses relationships without dead ends. A founder manages private documents and shares them through the product.
