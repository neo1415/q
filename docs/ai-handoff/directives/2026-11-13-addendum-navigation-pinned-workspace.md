# Addendum (owner, 13 Nov 2026): navigation completion and pinned workspace

Continues the consolidated directive and the earlier 13 Nov addenda.

1. **Finish and certify v85 navigation.** Use real positive and negative journeys. Navigation is complete only after live verification of:
   - explicit navigation, with deep links to page, record, tab and section;
   - contextual moves in the sidebar/workspace without "open";
   - no irrelevant moves or offers;
   - stable follow-ups.

   All safeguards stay: authorization, ambiguity, unsaved work, busy pane, collapsed pane, dedupe.

2. **Pinned workspace.** A Pin/Unpin control on the adjacent workspace, with a subtle pinned indicator. When the workspace is pinned:
   - automatic contextual navigation never changes the visible page;
   - Q may offer a destination instead;
   - an explicit navigation request offers a clear way to unpin and go;
   - manual navigation still works;
   - nothing else is affected (conversation, analysis, research, charts, actions);
   - it works on desktop and mobile.

   Build it on the existing workspace state and versioned contracts, with no unnecessary persistence. Assess the shared-contract impact first. No independent navigation system. If a substantial conflict appears, document it and continue other work.

   Tests: pin, unpin, explicit and contextual navigation, refresh, unsaved work, permission boundaries.

3. **Keep the whole programme moving.** Navigation work must not starve:
   - grounded claim extraction, company images, pitch media, rich results and research integration;
   - capability awareness, durable offers and real feature execution;
   - GateQ interviews, branding and end-to-end applications.
4. **Evaluation.**
   - The Anthropic eval credential is optional and never blocks other work. If it is used, report its cost.
   - Mark features complete only when their acceptance criteria are met. Keep implemented, tested, deployed and live-verified distinct.
   - Continue automatically. Do not stop for routine approvals or demo-data preparation.
