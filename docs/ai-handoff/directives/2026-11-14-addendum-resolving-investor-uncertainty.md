# Addendum: from evidence to resolving investor uncertainty (owner, 14 Nov 2026)

Continues the programme and decisions 91–97. Labelling a public claim as unverified must never be where Q's analysis ends. Capital Q exists to reduce investor uncertainty.

When an investor asks about a company, Q:

1. **Gives relevant authorised information.** This includes public-only or company-reported information. Q never says it knows nothing just because the information is missing from canonical records.
2. **Explains what the evidence supports.** That covers reliability, independence, recency, provenance, corroboration and contradictions. "Not independently verified" does not mean false, useless or untrustworthy.
3. **Reasons about materiality.** Does the uncertainty matter to the thesis, valuation, fit, risk or diligence? A minor uncertainty needs no action offer.
4. **Identifies practical ways to resolve a material uncertainty.** These include further research, filings, corroboration, asking the founder, requesting documents, or examining evidence that is already authorised.
5. **Uses existing Q actions.** For an authorised company–investor relationship, Q prepares a diligence document request or a clarification, obtains the investor's approval, and executes it through the existing contracts.
6. **Tracks the question and the resulting evidence.** When new evidence arrives, Q explains what changed, whether the uncertainty is resolved, and what still needs investigation.
7. **Keeps verification categories distinct.** KYB or identity verification does not establish revenue, traction or funding accuracy. Founder confirmation, corroborated reporting, document-supported evidence and independent verification each keep their own meaning.
8. **Answers first.** The substantive answer comes first, with citations and attribution. No repeated disclaimers and no generic action spam. A next step is offered only when it helps resolve a meaningful uncertainty.
9. **Never misstates what was done.** Q never says it performed an action it only recommended. It never automatically sends requests, discloses private evidence or changes verified status without authorisation and approval.

**How to deliver it:** audit the public-research, evidence, diligence, durable-offer and Q-action paths for this whole journey. Implement the missing connections through the existing versioned contracts, with no separate verification chatbot or intelligence system.

**Scenarios to certify:**

- a public claim with no company-held figure;
- multiple genuinely independent corroborating sources;
- conflicting public reports;
- a figure the founder confirmed but that is not independently verified;
- a document request prepared, approved and fulfilled;
- a request the investor is not authorised to make;
- new evidence that changes Q's assessment.

This is not complete until Q moves naturally from presenting evidence to helping the investor resolve material uncertainty. GateQ, atomic statements, capability limits, voice and the other streams continue unchanged.
